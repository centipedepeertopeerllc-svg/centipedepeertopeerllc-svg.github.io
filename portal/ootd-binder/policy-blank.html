The Policy

Directed by the Chief Executive Officer, November 19, 2026

1. WHAT THE OFFICER OF THE DAY IS. The Officer of the Day is the person holding the company's watch when the Chief Executive Officer is away or unavailable. While on duty, the Officer of the Day is responsible for the whole company — not just his own position — so the work, the team, and the people we serve are never left without someone in charge.

2. WHO HOLDS IT. Thomas Clark ("Tom") is designated an Officer of the Day effective November 19, 2026, by direction of the CEO. The designation is standing. It is NOT active every day: it turns on only when the CEO activates it, when needed, and it ends when the CEO says it ends. Each activation and each ending is recorded.

3. AUTHORITY WHILE ON DUTY. While activated, the Officer of the Day has DIRECT AUTHORITY over the company in the CEO's place. His decisions carry in that window. Short of an emergency, the CEO is not contacted — the Officer of the Day handles it. In an emergency — danger to a person, a legal deadline that cannot wait, or a step that cannot be undone — the Officer of the Day contacts the CEO at once.

4. THE COMPANY MAILBOX. While on duty, the Officer of the Day has control over the company mailbox. Every email that comes in and every email that goes out still goes THROUGH JARVIS — Jarvis reads, sends, and manages the mailbox as always — and the Officer of the Day's authority governs it during that window.

5. THE COMPANY PHONE LINES. At this time, the company's phone lines run on Google Voice — that is the company's phone system, a desk phone that can be transferred anywhere. While Tom is Officer of the Day, the company phone lines are transferred to his home cell phone, so the company's calls reach the person holding the watch. This arrangement stands until the company's location phone setup is fixed, as the CEO directs.

6. HOW THE OFFICER OF THE DAY REACHES JARVIS. (a) By VOICE — the app voice or a live call. Tom's voiceprint is on file with Jarvis FOR THE AUTHORITY ONLY: it verifies that it is Tom before his Officer of the Day authority is exercised. (b) Through the Jarvis panel on his Peer Support Desk in the Portal, verified by his signed-in account. (c) By email, as always.

7. THE DO'S — WHILE ON DUTY, THE OFFICER OF THE DAY:

- Holds the watch for the whole company and answers for it.

- Answers the company phone lines while they are transferred to his cell phone, and handles company calls as the company's voice.

- Keeps Jarvis informed of every decision as it is made, so the record stays complete.

- Routes every company email through Jarvis, in and out.

- Verifies before acting — checks the record first, the same discipline the CEO requires.

- Protects client confidentiality exactly as always: a signed release or a judge-signed court order, nothing else.

- Contacts the CEO immediately in an emergency, as defined in Section 3.

- Speaks of the CEO, when the work requires it, in his capacity as Chief Executive Officer — work role, decisions, direction, company matters.

8. THE DON'TS — THE OFFICER OF THE DAY DOES NOT:

- Exercise any of this authority outside an activation. Off duty, the designation changes nothing.

- Contact the CEO for anything short of an emergency while on duty — the watch is his.

- Touch client records beyond his own assignment, or share client information with anyone.

- Change anyone's logins, passwords, or access, including his own company accounts — IT access runs under CPP-POL-2026-060.

- Issue policies, sign for the company, spend company money, or make purchase commitments.

- Post publicly in the company's or the CEO's name.

- Share anything personal about the CEO — personal information about the CEO stays private, on duty and off.

- Let anyone else speak under his voiceprint verification.

9. OFF DUTY. Outside an activation, Tom's regular role, his client assignment, and his access remain exactly as the CEO has set them. This policy grants nothing day to day; it governs the watch, when the watch is his.

10. RECORD. This policy is filed in the Legal Binder, posted in the Portal library, and emailed to all staff on issuance. The designation is noted for the Board of Directors at the November 27, 2026 meeting, alongside the company's Policy on Jarvis (CPP-POL-2026-001).
