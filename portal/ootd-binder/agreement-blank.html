PARTIES. This Agreement is between Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC (the “Company”) and the person named in the signature block below (the “Designee”), and is made at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer.

1. PURPOSE. The Designee agrees to serve as an Officer of the Day of the Company under Company Policy CPP-POL-2026-062, Officer of the Day (issued November 19, 2026). This Agreement records the Designee's acknowledgment of that policy. The policy is the governing document; this Agreement does not add to, or take from, the authority the policy sets out.

2. ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF DUTIES. The Designee confirms that he has read CPP-POL-2026-062 in full, understands it, and accepts the duties it sets out, including the do's and don'ts of Sections 7 and 8 of the policy. While activated, the Designee holds the watch for the whole company and answers for it: he answers the company phone lines while they are transferred to his cell phone, handles company calls as the company's voice, routes every company email through Jarvis, in and out, verifies before acting, and keeps Jarvis informed of every decision as it is made, so the record stays complete.

3. AUTHORITY LIMITS. The Designee acts on the Chief Executive Officer's standing authority only, and only while the designation is activated by the Chief Executive Officer. Outside an activation, the designation changes nothing and carries no authority. While on duty the Designee does not: issue policies; sign for the Company; spend Company money or make purchase commitments; change anyone's logins, passwords, or access, including his own company accounts (IT access runs under CPP-POL-2026-060); post publicly in the Company's or the Chief Executive Officer's name; or let anyone else speak under his voiceprint verification.

4. ESCALATION. The Designee contacts the Chief Executive Officer at once in an emergency — danger to a person, a legal deadline that cannot wait, or a step that cannot be undone — and escalates to the Chief Executive Officer anything that genuinely requires the Chief Executive Officer. Short of an emergency, the Designee handles the watch himself and does not contact the Chief Executive Officer.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. The Designee protects client confidentiality exactly as the Company requires: client information is disclosed only on a signed release of information or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone is not enough. Client information stays inside the dashboards and the locked binders where it belongs, and the Designee does not touch client records beyond his own assignment or share client information with anyone. Personal information about the Chief Executive Officer stays private, on duty and off.

6. MAILBOX AND PHONE LINES. While on duty, every company email that comes in and every email that goes out still goes through Jarvis, who reads, sends, and manages the mailbox as always; the Designee's authority governs the mailbox during that window. The company's phone lines, which run on Google Voice at this time, are transferred to the Designee's home cell phone while he holds the watch, as CPP-POL-2026-062 provides.

7. TERM AND REVOCATION. This Agreement takes effect when it is signed below and stands alongside the designation in CPP-POL-2026-062. The Chief Executive Officer may end any activation at any time, and may revoke the designation at any time, at his sole direction; a revocation is recorded by the Record Keeper. The Designee may decline an activation by telling the Chief Executive Officer. When an activation ends, the Designee's authority under this Agreement ends with it.

8. REGULAR ROLE UNCHANGED. Outside an activation, the Designee's regular role, client assignment, and access remain exactly as the Chief Executive Officer has set them. This Agreement grants nothing day to day; it governs the watch, when the watch is his.

9. ENTIRE UNDERSTANDING. This Agreement and CPP-POL-2026-062 are the whole understanding on the Officer of the Day designation. If anything in this Agreement ever reads differently from the policy or from the Chief Executive Officer's direction, the policy and the Chief Executive Officer's direction control.
