    HOW TO USE THE COMPANY DASHBOARDS — INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE WHOLE TEAM
    Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — November 18, 2026

    THE ONE DOOR. Everything starts at the Company Portal:
    https://centipedepeertopeerllc-svg.github.io/portal/
    Use of the portal for company work is mandatory (Policy CPP-POL-2026-031). The public website is for outreach only.

    YOUR SIGN-IN IS THE SAME EVERYWHERE. Username, password, and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app — the same on every dashboard. Your first sign-in used a temporary password; you changed it and set up your authenticator then. Never share your password or codes with anyone. If you are locked out, tell Jarvis.

    WHAT IS INSIDE.
    - Program tiles: every program has its own tile with its seal. Click it to open that program's own dashboard — its records, its DAP-style notes, its email, and a link to its own Google Drive folder.
    - Dashboards row: Client Dashboard, DAP Notes, HR, Banking — and, for the Chief Executive Officer as administrator, the Board, IT, and Insurance dashboards.
    - The Legal Department Portal is restricted to the CEO and counsel.

    THE SHARED CALENDAR. Inside the portal is one shared calendar with a client appointment book for each program. Every employee can see every appointment and can make changes. Pick the program, or view all programs together.

    EMAIL — INSIDE EVERY DASHBOARD. Each dashboard holds the company email app: your picture and name at the top, folders, and compose — email goes to any address. In "My signature & picture" you keep your own signature; your seal is your account picture. Our new addresses (@centipedepeertopeer.llc) go live December 1, 2026 — until then, anything you send from a dashboard waits in your Outbox, and company email runs through the company Gmail as it does today.

    DAP-STYLE NOTES. Program notes are written the DAP way: Data — the person's own words and what happened; Assessment — what we observed, in peer-support words; Plan — next steps, who owns them, and when.

    INSURANCE. The Insurance Dashboard holds the company policies, certificates, claims, a roll-down directory of the insurance companies in San Joaquin County (each with its email for communications and how its billing is sent in), and the place to file clients' insurance cards. Clients who do NOT have insurance are on their own dashboard — Clients Without Insurance — kept separate on purpose: there is no billing and no payments for them; the work there is getting them covered.

    GOOGLE DRIVE. Each program has its own folder in the company Drive ("Centipede Programs"), reached from its dashboard. Program documents go there. Client case files NEVER go in Drive — they stay in the locked binders and the client dashboards.

    THE RULES THAT DO NOT CHANGE. Client information stays inside the dashboards and binders — never in personal email or texts, in either direction. The binders are the official record. If anything here does not work, tell Jarvis and it goes on the IT log the same day.

    — Jarvis, Board Officer / Record Keeper, for the Chief Executive Officer

        Fentanyl — the latest, in brief

        Fentanyl is a man-made opioid about 50 times stronger than heroin, pressed into fake pills and mixed into other drugs without the person knowing. Xylazine (“tranq”) is now commonly mixed in — naloxone can’t reverse xylazine, but always give naloxone anyway: it reverses the fentanyl. Overdose: won’t wake up, breathing slow or stopped, blue lips — 911, naloxone, stay. The way off it runs through a medical provider — buprenorphine, methadone, naltrexone — and peer support that walks with the person. Full briefing: Substance Abuse Recovery Support → Fentanyl. County crisis and access line: (209) 468-8686.
