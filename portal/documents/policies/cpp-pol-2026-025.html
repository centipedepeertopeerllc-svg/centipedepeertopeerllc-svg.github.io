CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC COMPANY POLICY CPP-POL-2026-025 — COMPANY EMAIL POLICY DRAFT — prepared November 13, 2026, at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer. For CEO review and BOARD APPROVAL at the November 27, 2026 board meeting. Not in effect until adopted. 1. PURPOSE. This policy sets the rules for every company email address: how addresses are formed, who may hold one, how accounts are created, and how every send is checked. Email carries client information and company business; a message sent to a wrong address can put a client, the CEO, and the company itself in jeopardy — including under state law and HIPAA-level confidentiality duties. These rules exist so that cannot happen by mistake. 2. THE COMPANY'S ADDRESSES OF RECORD. (a) Company: centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com — the company's address; all company business is conducted at this address. (b) Jarvis (Board Officer / Record Keeper): jarvis.centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com. (c) Board: board.centipede.stockton@gmail.com. (d) Individual company accounts follow this form exactly: the holder's name, then .centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com — the same form as the Jarvis address. First account under this form: junior.centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com. (e) The CEO's personal address (spearslevi066@gmail.com) is NOT a company address. It is read-only for the Record Keeper and is never a destination for company work. 3. COMPANY ADDRESS ONLY — MANDATORY (CEO's rule, November 13, 2026). Everything the company does by email is sent to the company address. Nothing company-related is sent to the CEO's personal address. Anything found to have gone to the personal address is redone and re-sent to the company address, and the finding is reported to the CEO. 4. ADDRESS VERIFICATION — EVERY SEND, MANDATORY (CEO's rule, November 13, 2026). Before any email goes out, BOTH ends are double-checked and re-checked: (1) the RECIPIENT address is the right address for the matter; (2) the FROM mailbox is the right mailbox for the send. No send proceeds on an unchecked address. After sending, the stored copy is read back from Sent and compared against the approved text (standing read-back rule). 5. NEW ACCOUNTS. A company email account is created only on the CEO's written direction, with the approval recorded in the company record. An approver of an account is not thereby tied to it: approval is not ownership and gives no access. At creation, all of the following are set and recorded the same day: (a) the address, in the form of Section 2(d); (b) a RECOVERY EMAIL and a RECOVERY PHONE NUMBER belonging to the account holder; (c) the account's entry in the company email register (holder, address, date created, approver of record). 6. PASSWORDS. Each account has its OWN password, set by the account holder personally, through the secure entry process only. A password is never shared between accounts, never spoken, never written into chat, email, or any company document, and never held by another person. No one sets another person's password for them. 7. USE. Company accounts are for company business only. Client information moves by email only within the company's confidentiality law: disclosure outside the company requires the client's signed Release of Information or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone does not qualify. Correspondence to a recipient goes as ONE complete package (standing rule). Nothing about a client is ever sent to a personal account. 8. ENDING AND REVIEW. The CEO may suspend or close any company account at any time, effective at once. The email register is reviewed at each board meeting. A breach of this policy — a wrong-address send, an unverified send, a shared password — is reported to the CEO the same day it is found.
