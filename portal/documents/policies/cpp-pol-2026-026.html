CPP-POL-2026-026 — Dashboard Emergency Access & Continuity Policy **Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — DRAFT, November 15, 2026** Drafted by Jarvis, Board Officer, at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer (Nov 15, 2026 — "I'm going to leave that one in your hands"). Pending CEO approval. 1. Purpose The company's dashboards (Client Dashboard, HR Dashboard, Banking Program) are working tools. The locked binders are the official record. This policy states what happens when a dashboard is down, not responding, or will not return a lookup, so that no one is ever blocked from information they need in an emergency. 2. Standing rule — the binders govern Dashboard data does not replace the binders. If a dashboard and a binder disagree, the binder is right, and the disagreement is logged and reconciled by Jarvis the same day it is found. 3. Emergency access protocol (when a dashboard is down or a lookup fails) 1. **Do not guess and do not rebuild from assumption.** A missing or unreachable profile is restored only from its binder, with the CEO, never filled in from memory. 2. **Go to the source of record:** - Client information → the client's locked binder (Client Binders). - Employee information, hours, and records → the HR Department binder and the person's HR file. - Money and balances → Mercury directly (Plaid read-only view) and the Banking Program binder; transactions are still assigned by the CEO only. 3. **Notify the CEO at once** — what is down, what was needed, and what source was used instead. No silent workarounds. 4. **Log the incident the same day**: date/time, dashboard affected, who needed access, what record was used, and the outcome — filed in the Employee Relations Record binder (for HR matters) or the dashboard↔binder sync log (for client/banking matters). 5. **Nothing is disclosed to any third party** during an outage that would not be disclosed normally. Client disclosure still requires a signed ROI or a judge-signed court order. An emergency does not lower that bar. 6. **When the dashboard returns**, Jarvis reconciles it against the binders before anyone relies on it, and reports the reconciliation to the CEO. 4. Maintenance Every two weeks, Jarvis runs a dashboard maintenance check on all dashboards: each one opens; sync counts reconcile with the binders and the banking record; member-view lookups are tested (including the CEO's and Junior's HR member views); needed updates are applied; findings are reported to the CEO. A failed check is treated as an incident under §3. 5. Access views Each dashboard carries two views: an administrator view (full access, CEO and Jarvis) and a member view (a person sees their own record only, by their record ID and PIN). A member-view failure is a defect, not a user error, and is repaired under §4. **Legal** — Counsel: Fluetsch and Fluetsch, Attorney at Law. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is fully dealt with counsel.
