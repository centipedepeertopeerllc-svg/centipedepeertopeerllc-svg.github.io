CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-037 — Women Helping Women Program Policy DRAFT — November 18, 2026 — for the Chief Executive Officer's approval; board notification November 27, 2026 1. PROGRAM. A peer-led, Christ-centered weekly women's circle — a full curriculum, shared meals and food bags, and mutual support among women. Facilitation is peer leadership, not counseling. 2. STATUS. In Development. The program is described publicly only with its true status label, under the company's public-programs standards. 3. LEAD. Program lead: Amanda Spears (invited — pending her decision). 4. SCOPE — PEER SUPPORT ONLY. This program is non-clinical peer support. Staff support, encourage, coach, mentor, navigate, connect to resources, share lived experience, facilitate groups, and advocate. Staff do not diagnose, treat, provide therapy or clinical counseling, or give medical, psychiatric, or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911. 5. RECORDS. The program's work is recorded in its own dashboard inside the Company Portal (records and DAP-style notes) and, where a member is a client, in the client's locked binder, which remains the official record. Client information follows the standing client-records rule: disclosure only on a signed client release of information or a judge-signed court order. 6. MONEY. Program purchases follow the purchase-order process (CPP-POL-2026-028 and CPP-POL-2026-029): no purchase order, no purchase. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company; nothing in this program's materials may state or imply nonprofit or charitable status. 7. PORTAL. The program is worked through the Company Portal and its own program dashboard (CPP-POL-2026-031). Its documents live in the program's own Google Drive folder; client case files never go in Drive. Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer — Record Keeper, November 18, 2026, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer. SECTION ADDED November 18, 2026 (CEO direction) — DOMESTIC VIOLENCE Many women who come to this circle carry domestic violence, past or present. The program's standing on it: 1. PEER SCOPE. Staff and facilitators listen, believe, and support. They do not investigate, do not counsel, and never contact an alleged abuser. Safety planning means helping her reach professional domestic violence services, not building the plan ourselves. 2. DANGER FIRST. If anyone is in danger right now, call 911. Referrals: the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233, and PREVAIL, San Joaquin County's domestic violence services. 3. NOTES. Domestic violence issues are noted in the program dashboard's dedicated Domestic Violence — Support Notes space (DV-#### numbers): what she shared (her words where possible), the support given, referrals, and follow-up. A safe address or a place she is hiding is NEVER written down. 4. CONFIDENTIALITY — THE STRICTEST IN THE PROGRAM. DV notes are disclosed only on her signed release of information or a judge-signed court order, under the company's standing client-records rule. They are never shared outside the program team. 5. REPORTING. Where California law requires a report (suspected child abuse or neglect; elder or dependent-adult abuse), staff report as the law requires and note the report in the DV note. Specifics are confirmed with the Chief Compliance Officer and the Legal Department.
