CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC Policy CPP-POL-2026-031 — Portal and Dashboard Use Policy (Mandatory Use) DRAFT — November 18, 2026 — for the Chief Executive Officer's approval; board notification November 27, 2026 1. PURPOSE. This policy writes into company policy what the Chief Executive Officer's company-wide update letter of November 18, 2026 directs: the Company Portal and the company dashboards are mandatory for company work, and client information lives inside them. 2. THE PORTAL IS THE FRONT DOOR. Every employee does company work through the Company Portal (https://centipedepeertopeerllc-svg.github.io/portal/). Use of the portal for company work is mandatory. The public company website is for outreach only and carries no internal work. 3. CLIENT INFORMATION. Client information is worked only inside the dashboards and apps built for it, and the locked client binders remain the official record. Client information is never put in personal email, personal text messages, or personal accounts — in either direction. Disclosure outside the company follows the standing client-records rule: a signed client release of information, or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone is not a court order. 4. ONE SIGN-IN, NEVER SHARED. Every dashboard uses the same sign-in per person — username, password, and authenticator two-factor code. A sign-in is personal. Passwords and codes are never shared, never written in any dashboard, binder, or note. 5. THE APPS AND WHAT EACH HOLDS. (a) Client Dashboard and DAP Notes — client records and peer-support notes. (b) Program dashboards — each program's own records and DAP-style notes, inside the portal. (c) Insurance Dashboard — company policies, certificates, claims, and clients' insurance cards. (d) Clients Without Insurance Dashboard — clients with no coverage and the work to get them covered, kept separate from the Insurance Dashboard. (e) Banking Dashboard — the banking program and program cards. (f) HR Dashboard — employee records. (g) Legal Department Portal — Legal Department filings, restricted to the Chief Executive Officer and counsel. (h) Board Dashboard — the Board's files register and program changes. (i) IT Department Dashboard — maintenance, infrastructure, computers, phones, and company systems. (j) Justice Support Program dashboard — the case register (court, docket and case numbers, attorney of record, dates) and government-ID verification copies; peer support only, never legal representation. 6. PROGRAM CHANGES. A change to a program entered on the Board Dashboard stands as proposed until the program's lead confirms it; the lead is notified from the dashboard of what changed, what needs to be fixed, and what needs to go on. 7. EMAIL. Every dashboard holds the company email app. Email is sent from company addresses only — from December 1, 2026, the @centipedepeertopeer.llc addresses. Each person keeps their own signature and picture in the app's signature space; a person's seal is their account picture (Policy register: IT Department binder, item 09). 8. CALENDARS. The portal's shared calendar holds one client appointment book per program. Every employee can see every appointment and can make changes. The standing calendar rules (CPP-POL-2026-027) still govern what appears on team calendars and on the public website calendar. 9. GOOGLE DRIVE. Each program has its own folder in the company Google Drive, reached from its dashboard. Program folders hold program documents. Client case files never go in Drive — they stay in the locked binders and the client dashboards. 10. RECORDS. Dashboards run in Local Mode until the company's shared-database step is completed on the Chief Executive Officer's word; until then the binders are the official record of everything the dashboards hold. Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer — Record Keeper, November 18, 2026, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer. ADDENDUM — November 18, 2026 (CEO direction): DOCUMENT NUMBERS. Every document that comes in or goes out of the portals and dashboards carries a document number, and email is numbered the same way: every email sent carries a CPP-EML-YYYY-#### number in its subject line, logged in the Email Document Register (~/workspace/email-register.txt). The dashboard email app assigns the number automatically when a message is queued. Existing dashboard series stand as the numbering for their records (LEG-, CHG-, POL-, COI-, CLM-, BILL-, CARD-, CASE-, VET-, UNI-, IT-, APT-).
