Document No. CPP-POL-2026-005 — Outreach No-Police-Involvement Advisory (number assigned November 12, 2026, under the CPP-POL numbering standard) Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — Outreach Policy: No Police Involvement in Independent Outreach **Status: APPROVED — COMPLETE POLICY, FINAL. Directed and approved by Levi Allen Spears, CEO, Nov 5, 2026, ~7:17-7:18 PM PST, in his words: "It's not a draft. It's a complete policy and approved." / "It's final." Supersedes the draft status recorded minutes earlier the same evening.** **Company: Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — a California for-profit limited liability company.** Policy text (approved, final) Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC's independent homeless outreach is peer-led peer support. It is not law enforcement. - No police officers take part in Centipede's independent outreach runs, and no police involvement is part of this outreach. - Centipede outreach workers do not conduct enforcement, sweeps, clearances, citations, or investigations, and do not act on behalf of any police department. - No advance notification: Stockton PD asked Centipede to let them know when Centipede is out on outreach. Levi's position, stated Nov 5, 2026: "I don't think we need to let them know." Under this final policy, Centipede does not notify police in advance of its independent outreach runs. - Outreach is voluntary connection only: peer support, encouragement, resource navigation, and referrals offered to people who choose to accept them. Nobody is required to talk to Centipede, accept supplies, or accept a referral. - This advisory applies, as directed Nov 5, 2026, to the Alpine Ave & West Lane outreach (Pixley Slough corridor; lookout name on record: Twiggy) and is to be advised in Centipede's outreach policies for independent outreach runs. - Emergency calls: in a genuine emergency — a medical crisis or an immediate danger to life — outreach workers call 911, the same as any member of the public. That is an emergency call, not police participation in outreach. Scope note (implementation record) This policy as approved governs Centipede's INDEPENDENT outreach. Centipede's existing peer handbook (delivered Sep 25-26, 2026, operational draft) contains separate joint-outreach protocols for the Stockton Police Department and San Joaquin County Probation, reflecting established professional, community-oriented working relationships with those agencies. Conforming those handbook sections to this final policy is an implementation task logged for follow-through; it does not change this policy's approved, final status.
