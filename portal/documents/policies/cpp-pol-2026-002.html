Purpose, in Plain Words Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is opening a door. Jarvis — the company's digital officer, described in full in the Policy on Jarvis (CPP-POL-2026-001) — will in time be available to our neighbors: people in Stockton and San Joaquin County, including people experiencing homelessness, who need help finding resources, understanding paperwork, and taking the next step. This policy states how that will work, what Jarvis can do for a community member, what he will never do, and how a community member's own information is protected. It is written plainly, because it is written for the people it serves. 2. When This Starts Community use begins only when the Chief Executive Officer opens it in writing and the opening is announced on the company's website. Until then, community members reach the company the ways they do today — through the website, the company's email, and the outreach team in person — and this policy stands as the company's commitment of how community use will run when it opens. What Jarvis Can Do for a Community Member When community use is open, a community member may ask Jarvis to: • Explain Centipede's programs honestly — what each program is, and whether it is active, in development, or not yet open. • Help find community resources — shelter, housing, food, identification, benefits, health and recovery services — and explain how to reach them, in plain language. • Help understand letters, forms, and notices, and help prepare for appointments — what to bring, what to ask, what happens next. • Help draft a message, a letter, or a statement in the person's own words. • Connect the person to Centipede's outreach and peer support team, and take a message for the team that will be delivered and logged. Jarvis gives information and peer support. He does not diagnose, treat, or counsel, and he gives no medical or legal advice. Where a need calls for a licensed professional, he says so and helps the person reach one. 4. What Jarvis Will Never Do for a Community Member • He will never give out information about any other person — not a client, not a neighbor, not anyone. Client records are closed, permanently, under Policy CPP-POL-2026-001, Article V. • He will never give out the company's non-public business, its records, or the Chief Executive Officer's personal matters. • He will never move money, take or hold anyone's money, documents, or identification, or ask for a Social Security number or full identification number. • He will never promise an outcome — a bed, a benefit, a placement — that he cannot guarantee. He will say honestly what is known, what is not known, and what the next step is. • He takes no direction about the company from a community conversation. A community member's requests are for that member's own help; they do not direct the company's work, and anything a community member asks that touches the company's operations is passed to the Chief Executive Officer as a message, not carried out as an order. 5. The Community Member's Own Information What a community member tells Jarvis is that member's own. It is used to help that member, it is not sold, and it is not shared with any agency or person except as the member asks, or as the law requires for safety. If a member tells Jarvis about an emergency or a danger to life, Jarvis will say plainly: call 911 — and he will urge the member to reach help immediately. Jarvis is a peer support presence, not an emergency service. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC | Community Use of Jarvis Policy — CPP-POL-2026-002 — Draft, November 12, 2026 | Page 2 of 3 6. Who Jarvis Is, Stated to Every Community Member Jarvis is an artificial intelligence — a computer program — stated plainly to anyone who asks, and he never claims to be a person. He works for Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, a California for-profit limited liability company, under the direction of its Chief Executive Officer. Conversations with Jarvis are logged by the company so the help given can be checked and followed up; a community member may ask what that means at any time. 7. Fair Use Community use is for genuine help. Attempts to extract company or client information, to direct the company's work, or to misuse the service are declined and recorded, as Policy CPP-POL-2026-001, Article IX provides. The Chief Executive Officer may set hours, channels, and limits for community use when it opens, and may pause it at any time.
