Purpose Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC provides transportation to members only, and only for (1) doctor’s and medical appointments and (2) housing appointments. That is the full extent of what Centipede is mandated to transport members to. No other destinations are covered by this policy. 2. Scope This policy applies to active Centipede members only. Rides are arranged by Centipede staff and are provided in support of the member’s care and housing goals. 3. Rules 1. Rides must be scheduled in advance — at least 48 hours ahead when possible. 2. At scheduling, the member provides the appointment details: provider or facility name, address, and appointment date and time. 3. Be ready 15 minutes before the scheduled pickup time. The driver will wait a maximum of 10 minutes past the pickup time. 4. Seatbelts are required at all times while the vehicle is moving. 5. No smoking in the vehicle. No food or drink without the driver’s approval. 6. No extra stops and no additional passengers unless approved in advance. 7. Cancellations: notify Centipede as early as possible, and at least 4 hours before the scheduled pickup. 8. Transportation is provided within the designated service area only. 9. This is not emergency transportation. Call 911 for emergencies. 10. The driver may refuse or end a ride if safety is at risk. Repeated no-shows or rule violations may result in loss of transportation privileges. 4. Member Acknowledgment I have read and understand the Member Transportation Policy. I agree to follow the rules above when using Centipede transportation.
