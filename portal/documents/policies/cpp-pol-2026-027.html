CPP-POL-2026-027 — Calendar Visibility & Public Calendar Policy **Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — DRAFT, November 17, 2026** Made by Jarvis, Board Officer, at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer (Nov 17, 2026). Pending CEO approval. Board to be notified at the November 27, 2026 meeting, per the standing rule that the board is notified of every company change. 1. Purpose This policy states where the company's calendar entries live, who can see them, and what may be shown to the public. It exists so that legal and company matters are visible to everyone who works with the company, while client and personal information is never published. 2. Team calendars — legal matters are visible to the team 1. When something legal happens — a filing, a verification, a demand, a reply, a deadline, correspondence with a court, an agency, or counsel — it is entered on the company's team calendars so everyone who works with the company can see it. 2. "All my calendars" (CEO's standing definition, Nov 17, 2026) means every calendar the company works from: the company calendar, the CEO's personal calendar, and the Jarvis calendar, which are shared with the team, plus the public website calendar under §3. 3. Team-calendar entries for legal matters may carry the full working detail the team needs: the matter, the parties' offices, dates, deadlines, and document numbers. Team calendars are internal and are shared only with the team. 4. The Centipede IT Schedule calendar is separate and carries IT items only; legal and program matters do not go on it, and IT items do not go on the other calendars. 3. The public calendar — the company website only, matters not names 1. The company's ONLY public calendar is the Work Calendar on the company website: https://centipedepeertopeerllc-svg.github.io/ 2. Public entries state the MATTER only — what the company is working on — and never individuals' names. No client names, no personal names, no case detail, no health information, no family matters. 3. Public entries use honest status labels (Active / In Development / Coming Soon — Pending Board Approval / Planned) and non-clinical language only, under the standing public-programs legal standards. 4. Public entries are program-level: public events, Officer of the Day dates, company milestones, funding deadlines, and board meeting dates. 4. What never goes public Client specifics of any kind; legal-strategy detail; the names of quiet partners; police-liaison content; anything whose publication could identify a client or a client's matter. If an entry cannot be stated without a name or case detail, it does not go on the public calendar at all — it stays on the team calendars under §2. 5. Maintenance and screening Jarvis updates the website Work Calendar daily from the team calendars, screens every public entry against §3 and §4 before it publishes, and keeps the "Current as of" date current. Corrections are factual, dated, and never rewrite past months' archive entries. **Legal** — Counsel: Fluetsch and Fluetsch, Attorney at Law. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is fully dealt with counsel.
