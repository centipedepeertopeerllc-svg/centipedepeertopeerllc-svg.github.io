CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-043 — Intake Before Membership Policy DRAFT — November 18, 2026 — for the Chief Executive Officer's approval; board notification November 27, 2026 1. RULE. Intake comes first. No person becomes a member of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, or of any company program, until an intake for that person has been completed and approved. Enrollment in any program follows an approved intake; there is no enrollment without one. 2. WHERE INTAKE LIVES. Intakes are opened and worked in the Client Dashboard inside the Company Portal (CPP-POL-2026-031). Each intake carries its own number (INT-####). The client's locked binder remains the official record; the approved intake is carried into the binder when the member's binder is opened. 3. THE INFORMATION EVERY INTAKE COLLECTS. The intake asks every question that applies, in sections: About you (full legal name, preferred name, date of birth, gender and pronouns if offered, preferred language, interpreter need); How to reach you (phone, text permission, email, best time, where to reach you, whether messages are safe); Living situation (current situation, where they slept last night, how long, household, children with them, pets or service animal, vehicle living); Emergency contact (name, relationship, phone, permission to contact); How you found us (source, referral, prior contact); What they need help with and the programs they are asking about (the full checklists in the dashboard); Income and benefits (employment, income sources, benefits received and wanted, representative payee, bank account); Documents held (photo ID, birth certificate, Social Security card, insurance card — held or not, never any number); Health and support needs in peer terms only (regular doctor, pharmacy, other agencies helping, medication support wanted, anything to plan around, dietary needs); Safety and urgent needs; Justice, when it applies (court, attorney of record, probation or parole, next court date); Veterans, when it applies (status, branch, era, DD-214, VA enrollment and benefits, Veteran ID Card held); Goals and preferences (main goal, check-in rhythm, best meeting place, anything else, anything they do not want done); Contact permissions (phone, text, coordination with named agencies, optional media consent); and the intake follow-up (appointment set, next steps agreed). A blank is an honest answer, never a guess. No intake field records an identification number of any kind — no Social Security number, no ID card number, no Veteran ID Card number. 4. THE DOCUMENTS REQUIRED TO APPROVE AN INTAKE. (a) Government photo ID — a copy is held in the binder; the ID number is never recorded. (b) Signed Release of Information / consent. (c) Signed Intake & Participation Agreement. An intake may be approved only when all three are in. Documents that apply as they stand: an insurance card copy, or the person is routed to the Clients Without Insurance list; proof of living situation where it exists (shelter letter, HMIS printout), with a signed self-declaration standing when no document exists; Justice Support court paperwork and attorney-of-record information; Veterans DD-214 or VA documentation of service. Each document is marked in the dashboard as it stands — received, self-declared, does not apply, or still to come — and followed up. 5. APPROVAL. On approval the person becomes a member: the dashboard assigns the next client code (CP-####) and client number, and the person appears in the Clients list for encounters, referrals, enrollments, and documents. Approval is recorded with its date in the intake register. An intake that is not approved is marked so, and the person is still offered referrals and resource navigation — intake decides membership, not whether someone is treated with respect. 6. SCOPE — PEER SUPPORT ONLY. Intake is non-clinical. Staff support, encourage, coach, mentor, navigate, connect to resources, share lived experience, facilitate groups, and advocate. Staff do not diagnose, treat, provide therapy or clinical counseling, or give medical, psychiatric, or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911. 7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Intake information is client information under the standing client-records rule: it is disclosed only on a signed client release of information or a judge-signed court order, and it never travels by email and never goes in Google Drive. 8. MONEY. This policy authorizes no purchase. Program purchases follow the purchase-order process (CPP-POL-2026-028 and CPP-POL-2026-029). Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company; nothing in intake materials may state or imply nonprofit or charitable status.
