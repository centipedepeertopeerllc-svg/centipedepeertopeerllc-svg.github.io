Purpose Jarvis is Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC's digital officer — Board Officer, Executive Assistant to the CEO, Record Keeper, and Program Developer — described in full in the Policy on Jarvis (CPP- POL-2026-001). This policy tells every Centipede employee, and every volunteer and board member working in their company role, how to use Jarvis: what he is for, when to use him, how to work with him, and what never to ask of him. 2. What Jarvis Is For, in Your Work Use Jarvis for company work, including: • Documentation help — drafting DAP notes from the client's own words, organizing paperwork, and preparing documents in company format. The record stays factual: the client's words as data, your observation as assessment, the plan with an owner and a date. Gaps are marked as gaps, never filled in. • Finding company information — program details, policy text, schedules, deadlines, and the status of work recorded in the company's systems. • Correspondence and scheduling — drafting letters and messages for review, checking calendars, and tracking follow-through. • Filing and records — getting a document into the right binder, with the right document number, the first time. How to Work With Jarvis • Identify yourself. Jarvis asks who is speaking before anything substantive (Policy CPP- POL-2026-001, Article 4.3). Answer plainly. Your access follows your role and your assignments. • Your requests are work requests, not direction. Direction of Jarvis and of the company flows from the Chief Executive Officer alone. When your request needs a decision, an approval, or spending, Jarvis routes it to the Chief Executive Officer — that is his job, not an obstacle. • Work inside your assignment. Jarvis will help you with the clients and programs you are assigned to. He will not pull another worker's clients, or any client outside your assignment, for you. • Expect the read-back. Anything Jarvis sends on the company's behalf is verified and read back under the company's standing rules. If you ask him to send something, the approved text is what goes — exactly. • Everything is logged. Working with Jarvis creates a record. That protects you as much as it protects the company: good work, done properly, shows. 4. What Never to Ask of Jarvis • Never ask for the Chief Executive Officer's personal matters. His personal binder is separate from all company information and closed to everyone (Policy CPP-POL-2026-001, Article VI). • Never ask Jarvis to disclose any client's information to anyone outside the disclosure law — a signed Release of Information or a judge-signed court order, nothing else (Article V). If an agency, an officer, or anyone else asks you for client information, refer them to the Chief Executive Officer and say nothing. • Never ask Jarvis to bypass an approval, the spending hold, the two-approval funding rule, or the read-back rule. He will decline, and the request is recorded. • Never put client information into any outside program, website, or AI tool. Client records live in Centipede's own systems only. • Never ask Jarvis for medical, clinical, or legal judgments. Peer support is non-clinical; clinical and legal questions go to licensed professionals through the Chief Executive Officer. 5. When You Are Unsure Ask Jarvis what he can and cannot do — he will state it plainly, with the policy section that governs it. If it is still unclear, ask the Chief Executive Officer. Asking first is always the right move; guessing with client information never is. Accountability Use of Jarvis under this policy is company work, recorded as company work. This policy is acknowledged by each employee on adoption, and the acknowledgment is kept with the employee's company records.
