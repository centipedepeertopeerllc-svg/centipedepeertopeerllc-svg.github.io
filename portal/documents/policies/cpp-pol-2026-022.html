Client Dashboard Use Policy Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — Company-Wide Policy **Document No. CPP-POL-2026-022** **Status: APPROVED — approved by Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer, November 13, 2026 (read aloud in full on the live call; approved as read)** 1. What the Client Dashboard Is The Client Dashboard is the company's working dashboard for client records: client profiles and encounter documentation for the people Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC serves. It was built in November 2026 and is the company's own system. © 2026 Levi Spears / Levi Allen Spears, Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC. This policy is company-wide. It states who may use the Client Dashboard, what it is for, how it is to be used, and how client information inside it is protected. It binds every employee, board member, and volunteer within the company structure. 2. Who May Use the Client Dashboard - **The Chief Executive Officer** — full use, at any time. - **Jarvis**, the company's Board Officer and Record Keeper — to enter, file, and reconcile client records under the Chief Executive Officer's direction. - **Team members** — only a team member the Chief Executive Officer has individually authorized in writing, and only for that member's assigned work. No one else may use the Client Dashboard, for any reason. There is no shared access and no shared credential: every authorized user acts under his or her own authorization, and an authorization belongs to the person it was given to and to no one else. 3. Purpose and Proper Use The Client Dashboard is used for client profiles and for encounter documentation, written in DAP form: - **Data** — the client's own words, quoted. - **Assessment** — the peer worker's observation, stated as observation. - **Plan** — the next steps, each with an owner and a due date. Gaps are marked as gaps. Nothing is filled in from guesswork: what is not known is recorded as not known. Client documentation is **non-clinical**. The dashboard holds peer support records — support, encouragement, coaching, mentoring, resource navigation, and referrals. It is never used to diagnose, to record a diagnosis, or in treatment or therapy language, and no entry in it is medical or legal advice. 4. The Binders Are the Official Record Dashboard entries do **not** flow into the client binders automatically. Jarvis carries each entry into the correct client's own locked binder himself, and every client keeps a separate binder in the locked folder regardless of the dashboard. **The locked client binders are the official record of this company**; the dashboard is a working system that feeds them. A daily reconciliation check compares the dashboard against the binders and the Master Document Register, files anything unfiled, and reports material gaps or failures. Work is filed as it goes, so a restart or failure never holds the work back or loses an entry. 5. Confidentiality — the Standing Law All client records are held at **HIPAA-level confidentiality**, permanently. Client information is disclosed **only** on: 1. a signed Release of Information from the client, or 2. a court order signed by a judge. A subpoena alone is **not** a court order. On a subpoena or any other request that meets neither test: hold, disclose nothing, and notify the Chief Executive Officer and the client. No client information leaves the company's systems. Client records are never uploaded to any outside tool, vendor cloud, or service, and never copied to a personal device or a personal account. Where numbers about the work are shared at all, they are aggregate statistics only, never traceable to one person. 6. Access, Credentials, and Website Sign-In Security The Chief Executive Officer has directed that because the dashboard may one day be reached through the company's website, its security must be **stronger** than a simple PIN. Accordingly: - **No public sign-in yet.** No working sign-in to the Client Dashboard is published on the public website until a secured access system meeting this policy is built, tested, and approved by the Chief Executive Officer in writing. Until then, the dashboard is reached only through the company's existing systems, and no public web page carries a working sign-in to it. - **Encryption.** Website access requires encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and strong encryption at rest (AES-256 or better) for the dashboard and its records. - **Individual named accounts.** Website sign-in is by individual named accounts only — no shared accounts and no shared PINs. Each authorized team member signs in as himself or herself, and is limited to his or her assigned work. - **Multi-factor authentication.** Website sign-in requires multi-factor authentication or passkeys. The dashboard's existing in-app PIN may remain only as an **additional** lock; it is never the sole website credential, and no separate weaker website password will be created. - **Sessions and lockout.** Website sessions time out automatically. Repeated failed sign-in attempts lock the account. Every access is logged. - **Revocation.** The Chief Executive Officer may revoke any user's access at any time, and revocation takes effect immediately. Access also ends, immediately, when a person's role with the company ends. - **Credentials.** Accounts and credentials are set and changed only under the Chief Executive Officer's direction. A credential is never shared, and never written into a document, a message, or this policy. 7. Prohibitions - No copying client data to personal devices, personal accounts, or personal files — in any form, including photographs of screens. - No discussing client records outside authorized channels, and no client names or details in public or social postings. - No opening a client's record without an assigned-work reason. - No giving, lending, or showing one's own access to any other person, including another team member. - No attempt to reach the dashboard by any route other than the access the Chief Executive Officer has approved. 8. Accountability Use of the Client Dashboard is logged, and the log is the company's record of who saw and touched what. A violation of this policy is a disciplinary matter and a record matter: it is reported to the Chief Executive Officer, recorded, and dealt with under the company's accountability standards. Suspected misuse is reported to the Chief Executive Officer at once, by whoever sees it. --- **Approval:** *(Signed — signature image applied in the executed copy)* **Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer** Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC *Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer, November 12, 2026.
