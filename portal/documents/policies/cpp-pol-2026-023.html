Centipede Banking Program Use Policy Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — Company-Wide Policy **Document No. CPP-POL-2026-023** **Status: APPROVED — approved by Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer, November 13, 2026 (read aloud in full on the live call; approved as read)** 1. What the Centipede Banking Program Is The Centipede Banking Program is the company's own program-card banking system: the company's program cards, their balances and transactions, and the ledger that assigns each transaction to its program. © 2026 Levi Spears / Levi Allen Spears, Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC. The program holds the company's accounts at Mercury through a read-only connection by way of Plaid. Mercury is named in **plain text only** in this program and everywhere in the company — never its logo or wordmark styling. Mercury is a technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided by Mercury's partner banks, and nothing in this program is endorsed by Mercury. This policy is company-wide. It states who may use the Banking Program — and who may not — how money moves through it, and the rules that govern every entry. 2. Sole User — the Chief Executive Officer Alone The Banking Program is for the **Chief Executive Officer alone**. Jarvis operates it as Record Keeper, under the Chief Executive Officer's direction — entering what the Chief Executive Officer decides, keeping the ledger, and reporting. Jarvis holds no independent authority in it. No team member, no board member, and no other person has access to the Banking Program — through the app, through any future website door, or by any other route. There are no exceptions to this section except a written amendment to this policy by the Chief Executive Officer. 3. The Money Path The company's money path stands as the Chief Executive Officer established it: **funds are deposited into the company's account at Chase first, and are then separated into Mercury.** Deposits may therefore reach the Mercury register after a Chase first stop. Every movement is recorded on the ledger as it happens, so the path of each dollar can be shown from the record. 4. The Manual Assignment Rule Synced Mercury transactions arrive in the program badged as Mercury-synced, in **Pending Review**. **Nothing posts to a program card on its own.** The Chief Executive Officer assigns each transaction himself — to its program card and its tax category — and only an assigned transaction stands posted. Automatic assignment may be adopted **only** if the Chief Executive Officer and the board later decide it together, and any such change is documented before it takes effect. 5. Read-Only Banking — Jarvis Moves No Money The Plaid connection is **read-only**: it reads balances and transactions. Jarvis does not move money in any account, and the program makes no payments. Money moves only by the Chief Executive Officer's own hand, outside Jarvis's operation of this system. No full card numbers, no card verification codes, and no expiry dates are ever recorded in the program or anywhere in the company's records — **last four digits only**. 6. The Tax Center A Tax Center is built inside the Banking Program, on the same ledger: every posted entry appears in the tax view automatically. The only human step is the tax category; an entry without one stands flagged **"Needs tax category"** until the Chief Executive Officer assigns it. Tax categories follow the standard IRS and California structure. They are **working categories only**: a licensed CPA or tax advisor confirms the categories before any filing use. The system tracks and categorizes; **it is not tax advice.** 7. Access, Credentials, and Website Sign-In Security The Chief Executive Officer has directed that because this program may one day be reached through the company's website, its security must be **stronger** than a simple PIN. Accordingly: - **No public sign-in yet.** No working sign-in to the Banking Program is published on the public website until a secured access system meeting this policy is built, tested, and approved by the Chief Executive Officer in writing. Until then, the program is reached only through the company's existing systems. - **Encryption.** Website access requires encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and strong encryption at rest (AES-256 or better). - **One named account.** Website sign-in, if it is ever opened, is for the Chief Executive Officer alone, under his own named account, with the **strongest verification the system supports** — multi-factor authentication or a passkey, required at every sign-in. No shared accounts and no shared PINs exist in this program. The program's existing in-app PIN may remain only as an **additional** lock; it is never the sole website credential, and no separate weaker website password will be created. - **Sessions and lockout.** Website sessions time out automatically. Repeated failed sign-in attempts lock access. Every access is logged in full. - **Revocation.** The Chief Executive Officer may revoke or reset any access to this program at any time, effective immediately. - **Credentials.** Credentials are set and changed only under the Chief Executive Officer's direction. A credential is never shared, and never written into a document, a message, or this policy. 8. Reporting A daily sync check refreshes the program's recorded balances from Mercury by way of Plaid and reports to the Chief Executive Officer: the balances, the count of transactions pending his assignment, and the count of entries needing a tax category. If a full sync cannot be completed, the report says so plainly — an unavailable count is reported as unavailable, never as zero. 9. Prohibitions and Accountability - No person other than the Chief Executive Officer may use, open, or attempt to open the Banking Program. **Any other person's attempt to access it is recorded and reported to the Chief Executive Officer at once.** - No recording of full card numbers, verification codes, or expiry dates — anywhere, by anyone. - No posting of any transaction except by the Chief Executive Officer's own assignment under Section 4. - No change to the program's accounts, cards, connections, or credentials except under the Chief Executive Officer's direction. Use of the Banking Program is logged, and the log is the company's record. A violation of this policy is a disciplinary matter and a record matter, reported to the Chief Executive Officer and recorded. --- **Approval:** *(Signed — signature image applied in the executed copy)* **Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer** Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC *Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer, November 12, 2026.
