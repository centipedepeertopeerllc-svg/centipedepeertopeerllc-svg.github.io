CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-030 — COMPLIANCE PROGRAM AND CHIEF COMPLIANCE OFFICER POLICY DRAFT — prepared November 18, 2026, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer ("if a department doesn't have the policy, make one"). For CEO approval; board notification November 27, 2026. 1. PURPOSE. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC runs by the book. This policy establishes the company's compliance function under a Chief Compliance Officer (CCO), so that every department's work follows the company's policies, its confidentiality rules, and the law that applies to it. 2. THE OFFICE. The Chief Compliance Officer is appointed by, and reports to, the Chief Executive Officer. First holder: Tammy Lopazzo, appointed November 17, 2026, Employee ID 2007. 3. DUTIES. The CCO: (a) Policy oversight — knows the company's policies, tracks their status (draft or adopted), and reports gaps and needed updates to the CEO. (b) Client-records confidentiality — upholds the standing rule: no client information is disclosed to anyone without (1) a signed client release of information, or (2) a judge-signed court order. A subpoena alone is NOT a court order. Client records carry HIPAA-level confidentiality at all times. (c) Email and records practices — compliance with CPP-POL-2026-025 (Email Policy), including its encryption section, and with the company's binder and records discipline. (d) Financial process compliance — the purchase-order process under CPP-POL-2026-028 and CPP-POL-2026-029: NO PO, NO PURCHASE; the $250 CEO countersignature; client purchase orders and company purchase orders kept as separate records, never mixed. (e) Deadlines and filings — board, state, and program deadlines tracked with lead time, never at the last minute. (f) Public-material standards — every public program listing carries its status label (Active / In Development / Coming Soon — Pending Board Approval / Planned), non-clinical language only, and the company's for-profit status stated plainly. (g) Reporting — any compliance problem found is reported to the CEO the same day, in writing, with the record cited. 4. AUTHORITY. The CCO may ask any department for its records on a compliance question, and may require a practice to pause until the CEO decides where continuing it would break a policy or a confidentiality rule. The CCO does not overrule the CEO. 5. LIMITS. The CCO is not legal counsel and gives no legal advice; legal questions go to the Legal Department and to counsel as the CEO directs. Compliance work is non-clinical and does not decide client services, which stay with the CEO and program staff. 6. COORDINATION AND RECORDS. The CCO works with the Legal Department — the Legal Binder (CPP-BND-2026-001) is the company's complete legal record — and with Jarvis as Record Keeper. Every compliance finding is written and filed the same day it is made. 7. REVIEW. This policy is reviewed quarterly and after every board meeting.
