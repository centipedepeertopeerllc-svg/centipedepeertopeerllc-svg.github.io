ADDENDUM A — MAIL RECEIPT FOR HOMELESS INDIVIDUALS SERVED BY THE COMPANY — DRAFT Addendum to the Home Office Permission Agreement between Craig C. Gilmore (Owner) and Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, November 13, 2026. Carries the company's Mail Receipt Policy and Rules: CPP-POL-2026-024 (DRAFT — pending CEO approval). STATUS: DRAFT — for the CEO's review; signed with the Agreement. PURPOSE. Some individuals served by Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC through its Homeless Outreach program have no mailing address. Without an address, a person cannot receive an ID, benefits letters, court notices, or job mail. Under the Owner's permission in the Agreement, the Company may receive mail for such a person at 717 E. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207, strictly under these rules. THE RULES 1. SIGNED AUTHORIZATION FIRST. No mail is accepted for any person until that person has signed the Company's Mail Receipt Authorization, naming the person, the address, and these rules. One person, one authorization. No authorization, no mail service. 2. HOW MAIL IS ADDRESSED. Mail for a participating individual is addressed to the individual's name, c/o Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, 717 E. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207. 3. LOGGED ON ARRIVAL. Every piece received is logged the day it arrives: date, the name it is addressed to, and the sender as shown on the envelope. The log is a company record, kept by the Record Keeper. 4. NEVER OPENED. The Company never opens another person's mail. Pieces are held sealed, exactly as received. Mail is opened by the addressee alone. 5. HELD SECURELY. Held mail is kept in a locked place at the home office, separate from household and company mail, and is handled as confidential client information under the company's confidentiality rules. 6. PICKUP BY THE PERSON THEMSELVES. Mail is handed only to the person it is addressed to, in person, with identification where the person has it, or verified by the Chief Executive Officer where they do not. Pickup is signed for in the log. Mail is never handed to a third party without the addressee's written instruction. 7. HOLD PERIOD. Mail is held thirty (30) days from arrival. The Company makes reasonable efforts through its outreach contacts to let the person know mail is waiting. Mail unclaimed after thirty days is returned to the sender, marked "Not at this address — return to sender," and the return is logged. 8. WHAT IS NOT ACCEPTED. No packages requiring a signature on the person's behalf, no medication, no cash or valuables sent through the mail, and nothing unlawful. Anything of that kind that arrives is logged and returned to the carrier or sender. 9. NO FEE. This service is free. Nothing is charged to the individual, ever. 10. NO RIGHTS CREATED. Receiving mail at this address creates NO residency, NO tenancy, NO occupancy right, and no claim of any kind against the property or the Owner, for the individual or for anyone else. It is a mailing courtesy under the Owner's permission, nothing more. It is not to be written or spoken of as the individual's home address of residence. 11. ENDING. The Company may end mail receipt for any person at any time — when the person leaves the program, when the person is housed and has their own address, on misuse, or on the Chief Executive Officer's decision — and will tell the person in person or through outreach, and note the ending in the log. On the ending of the Agreement itself, mail receipt winds down within the notice period and senders are redirected by return marking. 12. THE OWNER'S POSITION. The Owner's only part in this addendum is the permission itself. The Owner does not handle, hold, log, or answer for any individual's mail. All handling is the Company's, and all responsibility for the handling is the Company's.
