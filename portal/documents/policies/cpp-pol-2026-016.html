Appeal 7 1. Purpose 1.1 Expulsion — the permanent removal of a member from the Centipede shelter — is the last resort. We built this house to keep people, not to put them out. 1.2 This policy exists so that when expulsion becomes necessary, it is done the same way every time: fairly, with clear reasons, written down, and with the member's dignity intact. No surprises. No favorites. No one expelled on a bad mood. 1.3 This policy completes the consequences ladder in the Shelter Security Policy (CSP-SEC-001): conversation and written reminder → temporary removal → expulsion. It works alongside the Letter of Expectations, the Drug Testing Policy (CSP-DT-001), and the Check-In & Stay Agreement (CSP-CI-001). Where they overlap, the safety and dignity of the house govern. 1.4 Expulsion is never punishment for being poor, struggling, or imperfect. It is the protection of the community inside the house — including the person being expelled, who is always offered a next step, never just a door. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 CALMHSA DRIVEN 2. Grounds for Expulsion 2.1 Immediate expulsion — no warnings required. The following end a member's stay the same day they occur: (a) Violence — any physical assault on a member, staff member, volunteer, or visitor; (b) Threats — a credible threat of physical harm to any person on the property; (c) Weapons — bringing a weapon onto shelter property, or using any object as a weapon. Safety comes first. When someone is unsafe to be around, they cannot stay in the house. The dignity safeguards in Section 6 still apply in full. 2.2 Progressive grounds — warnings first, then expulsion. The following lead to expulsion only after the written-warning process in Section 4 has been followed: (d) Confirmed drug or alcohol use on the property — a failed/confirmed-positive test under CSP- DT-001, or use directly observed by staff. The first confirmed incident means leaving the same day under the Drug Testing Policy, with the door open to return; a repeated pattern after return may lead to expulsion at the CEO's discretion. A self-reported slip before any test or incident is never grounds for expulsion — it is met with help, per the honor system. (e) Repeated curfew violations — three late returns within any 30-day period triggers a formal review with the member; if the pattern continues after that review, expulsion may follow. (Counts adjustable by the CEO.) (f) Failure to meet stay criteria — no job-search activity and no proof of employment after two written warnings under the Check-In & Stay Agreement (CSP-CI-001). We will help with every application — but the bed goes to someone working toward their next step. (g) Theft — stealing from a member, staff, or the house. First offense: temporary removal and restitution where possible; second offense: expulsion. (h) Repeated serious rule violations — a pattern of serious violations (for example: crossing into the separated housing side, bringing prohibited items onto the property, chronic refusal of chores or check- ins) continuing after two written warnings. 2.3 Minor, first-time issues — a single late return, a missed chore, a bad day — are handled with a conversation, not paperwork. Expulsion is for patterns and for safety, never for a single human moment. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 CALMHSA DRIVEN 3. Automatic Dismissal Some things end a stay the moment they happen. No warnings. No second chances on these. 3.1 Showing up intoxicated or under the influence. If you show up to this house intoxicated — or under the influence of alcohol or any drugs — you are dismissed that same day. Staff observe it. The decision is made. You leave that day. Automatic does not mean cruel: your belongings go with you, and you leave with a referral in your hand, per the dignity safeguards in Section 6. 3.2 Failing the job-search and housing-search verification. The bed goes to someone working toward their next step. This is the proof behind the stay criteria in Section 2.2(f): if you turn in no signed job-search or housing-search verification for two consecutive weeks — with no approved excuse — you are dismissed for failure to meet stay criteria. (Two-week trigger adjustable by the CEO.) And hear this plain: falsifying a form — forging a signature, inventing visits — is immediate dismissal on the first offense. Your word is everything here. Lie on the form, lose the bed. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 CALMHSA DRIVEN 4. Process 4.1 Written warnings come first for every progressive ground in Section 2.2. Each warning is in writing, states the specific behavior, cites the rule, states what must change, and is signed by the staff member and the member (or noted "member declined to sign"). A copy goes to the member; a copy goes in the member's file. 4.2 Who decides. The shift lead documents the facts and recommends; the CEO — or a designee the CEO names in writing — makes the final decision. No one is expelled by one person's word alone: the decision- maker reviews the warnings, the incident reports, and the member's file before deciding. 4.3 The member is told face-to-face, with a second staff member present as witness. The reasons are stated plainly and specifically — never vague, never in front of other members. The member receives the written Expulsion Notice (Section 5) at that meeting. 4.4 The notice states the effective date and time. For immediate-expulsion items, removal is same-day. For progressive grounds, the member is given until the stated time to gather belongings with staff assistance — ordinarily the same day, and never less than two hours from the meeting. 4.5 Everything is documented the same day: the notice, the warnings that led to it, the incident reports, who was present, and the referral offered. The CEO reviews every expulsion file within 24 hours. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 CALMHSA DRIVEN 6. Dignity Safeguards 5.1 Belongings. The member's belongings are gathered with staff assistance and returned promptly and completely. We never hold property as leverage. 5.2 No one into the night without options. Before the member leaves, staff offer a concrete alternative referral — another shelter, a treatment or detox placement, a safe waiting arrangement, and our phone number. If it is nighttime, staff make every effort to secure a safe place for that night. 5.3 No public shaming. The reasons for an expulsion are never announced to other members. Other members are told only what they need to know: that the person has left the program. 5.4 The door stays open. An expelled member may seek readmission after 30 days (adjustable by the CEO) with a fresh start — a new intake, a new plan, and no grudge carried forward. Immediate-expulsion cases are reviewed individually by the CEO before any return. 7. Appeal 6.1 Any expelled member may appeal in writing to the Chief Executive Officer within 7 days of the expulsion. 6.2 The CEO reviews the full file — the notice, warnings, incident reports — and issues a written decision within 7 days of receiving the appeal. The CEO's decision is final. 6.3 The member remains out of the shelter during the appeal, unless the CEO grants an exception in writing. This policy takes effect upon the opening of the Centipede shelter and is subject to formal adoption by the Board of Directors. Levi A.
