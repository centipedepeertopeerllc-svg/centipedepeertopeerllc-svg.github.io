CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC Company Policy CPP-POL-2026-059 — ISSUED November 19, 2026 Branded Email in the Portal Policy Directed by the Chief Executive Officer, November 19, 2026 1. PURPOSE. Email done through the Company Portal and the dashboards must look like Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC the moment anyone sees it. This policy establishes the letterhead-style branded frame that every Portal email carries, and the automatic per-employee signature plate that identifies the sender, so that an email composed in the Portal, an email queued or sent from it, and an email opened in it all wear the same company branding — the same standard the company's letterhead carries on paper. 2. WHERE IT LIVES. The branding is built into the dashboards' email app (email-panel.js), which appears inside the Company Portal and every program and department dashboard under the Portal and Dashboard Use Policy (CPP-POL-2026-031). The email app is a local app: composed mail is numbered automatically (CPP-EML-YYYY-####, per the Document Numbers standard) and held in the sender's Outbox until the company mailboxes at @centipedepeertopeer.llc go live on December 1, 2026; until then, company email runs through the company Gmail as it does today. This policy governs the branding of the email, not the mailboxes. 3. THE FRAME. Every email composed in the app is stored and shown with the company frame: (a) a header in the letterhead style — the company logo, "Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC," the green-to-gold band, and the company motto, "Family Run Company"; (b) a clean body area carrying the message; (c) the sender's signature plate (Section 4); and (d) a footer carrying the full company block: 717 E. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207; Cell: (209) 871-3939 | Office: (209) 625-9863 or (209) 426-0964; Email: centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com; Board: board.centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com; Web: www.centipedepeertopeer.llc; LIC: 0714781; and the Legal line at the bottom: "Legal — Counsel: Fluetsch and Fluetsch, Attorney at Law. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is fully dealt with counsel." The frame appears in the compose preview, in the stored copy of every queued or sent email, and in the reading view when any stored email is opened in the app. 4. THE SIGNATURE PLATE. Each employee's signature plate is generated automatically from the person's identity in the email app — the employee does nothing to build it and cannot leave it off. A plate carries: the employee's name; their title; their own seal; their company email address; and their phone number where one is on record. Plate data is matched to the Email Signature Banner Register (November 18, 2026): the same names, titles, seals, addresses, and phone numbers as the person's signature banner. A person's separate free-text signature space in the app (their own signature wording and picture, kept since November 18, 2026) is unchanged by this policy; the plate is added by the app on top of it. 5. THE MANDATED REPORTER SEAL — CERTIFIED ONLY. The Mandated Reporter seal appears on a signature plate only when that employee is a certified Mandated Reporter under CPP-POL-2026-048, consistent with the seal-use rule in CPP-POL-2026-049. As of this policy's drafting, the only certified person is Levi Allen Spears (certificate CPP-MR-2026-001), and his plate carries the seal with the caption "Certified Mandated Reporter." Thomas Clark III's certificate (CPP-MR-2026-002) is held unsigned pending his training, so his plate carries no Mandated Reporter seal until the certificate issues. The Record Keeper updates the plate data the same day any certificate issues; where certification is uncertain, the seal is left off — never the other way. 6. THE WEB ADDRESS STANDARD. The only web address the frame, the plates, and any Portal email may carry is www.centipedepeertopeer.llc. The former address is retired and scrubbed (November 19, 2026) and must not appear in any email, plate, or frame. Any template or stored draft found carrying the retired address is corrected before use. 7. GMAIL SIGNATURES — SEPARATE, UNCHANGED. This policy governs email inside the Portal only. Signatures in Gmail itself remain governed by the existing email signature banner standard (the banners built and registered November 18, 2026, matched in Section 4). Installation of those banners into Gmail settings is held until the December 1, 2026 domain and email upgrade, per the CEO's direction of November 19, 2026, and proceeds then under the migration plan. 8. NO CLIENT INFORMATION. Portal email branding carries company and employee information only. Nothing in the frame or a plate ever carries client information, and this policy does not change where client matters are recorded — client records stay in the locked client binders, never in the Portal. Issued November 19, 2026, by direction of the Chief Executive Officer. Filed in the Legal Binder, posted in the Portal library, and emailed to all staff on issuance.
