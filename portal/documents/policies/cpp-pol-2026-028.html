CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-028 — PURCHASE ORDER APPROVAL AND TIMING POLICY Adopted at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer, November 17, 2026. Board notification: November 27, 2026 board meeting. Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer / Record Keeper. 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE. Every purchase the company makes — company, program, or client — is made on a Purchase Order (Form CPP-PO). NO PO, NO PURCHASE. This policy sets who approves Purchase Orders, how long approval takes, and how the three kinds of PO are kept apart. 2. THE THREE KINDS OF PO — ALWAYS SEPARATE. (a) Company purchase: supplies, services, equipment, printing, and operating needs of the company itself. A company PO never carries client information. (b) Program purchase: spending for a named company program, from that program's funds. (c) CLIENT purchase: a purchase made for an individual client. A client PO always completes the Client Information section of the form (client name, client ID / binder number, program, the need, and the justification) and is a confidential record. Company and program POs are filed in the PO binder (event POs in the Upcoming Events POs and Authorizations binder). Client POs are filed in the Client POs binder AND in the client's own binder, and are entered in the client dashboard. The kinds are never mixed in one binder or on one PO. 3. WHO APPROVES. Until a Financial Officer is appointed under the Financial Officer position (approved November 17, 2026), the acting approver of all Purchase Orders is ERIC JACOBSON, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer (November 17, 2026). When a Financial Officer is appointed, PO approval passes to the Financial Officer and this policy is amended to say so. The approver approves a PO only when the funds are identified (which program, card, or account pays), the price is documented, and the purchase fits its purpose. 4. CEO COUNTERSIGNATURE. Every PO above $250 — the limit set by the CEO on November 17, 2026 — also requires the Chief Executive Officer's countersignature before the order is placed. 5. APPROVAL TIMING. (a) STANDARD: approval of a complete PO takes up to TWO WEEKS from the day the complete PO reaches the approver. An incomplete PO is returned for completion, and the clock runs from the completed PO. (b) EMERGENCY: an emergency PO is approved within up to TWO BUSINESS DAYS. A PO is an emergency when delay would endanger the health or safety of a client or member, when an essential service to a home is out (heat, cooling, water, power), or when a deadline cannot move. The requester marks the PO "EMERGENCY" and states the ground on its face. (c) Approval is approval of the paperwork. Payment is made through the company's banking channels as the CEO directs, and every payment traces to an approved PO. 6. CLIENT PO CONFIDENTIALITY. A client PO never carries a Social Security number or a full ID number. Client information on a PO is disclosed outside the company only on the client's signed Release of Information or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone does not qualify. 7. RECORDS. Every approved PO is kept with its invoice or receipt and its completed receipt section: the financial file (the binder for its kind), the client's binder for a client PO, and the client dashboard for a client PO. The PO register is updated for every PO. Adopted: Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer — November 17, 2026.
