No Extension Without Approval 3 6. Appeals 3 7. Adoption 3 Extension Request Form 4 1. The 52-Day Limit Every shelter stay is limited to 52 days. The shelter is a bridge, not a destination — the limit keeps beds turning so the next person sleeping outside tonight gets the same chance you got. Fifty-two days is enough time to do real work: get your documents in order, find work or steady income, and line up your next housing — with our staff walking beside you the whole way. The limit is not a punishment. It is how we make sure the door stays open for everyone. 2. How the Count Works • Day 1 is your check-in date. The count runs 52 consecutive calendar days from there. • Your exit date is written on your check-in paperwork and posted where you can see it. No surprises. • If you leave the shelter early and return later, a new stay begins with a new count, subject to bed availability and staff approval. • Staff will remind you at day 30 and day 45 where you stand, so you always know your timeline. 3. Requesting an Extension If you need more time, you may ask for it — but you must ask before day 45 of your stay. Here is how: • Fill out the Extension Request Form (page 4 of this document) completely and honestly. • Turn it in to your case manager no later than day 45. Late requests are not accepted except in a genuine emergency, decided by the case manager. • On the form, show your progress: what you have done on your employment and housing plan, how you have followed the house rules, and why more time will make the difference. Asking early is part of working your plan. Waiting until day 51 to ask tells us the plan is not moving — and the answer will reflect that. The Case Manager’s Decision Your case manager reviews every extension request and weighs three things: • Progress — are you working your employment and housing plan? Applications filed, interviews attended, documents gathered. • Conduct — have you followed the house rules: the expectations letter, the security policy, the drug testing agreement? • Continued need — will more time genuinely move you closer to housing, or is the stay standing still? You receive the decision in writing on the Extension Request Form. If approved, the form states your new exit date. Approvals grant one 30-day extension (adjustable by the Chief Executive Officer). There are no second extensions except by direct CEO approval in extraordinary circumstances. 5. No Extension Without Approval Plain and simple: nobody stays past day 52 without a signed, approved Extension Request Form. Not staff, not volunteers, not friends of the house — no one can extend a stay except the case manager, in writing, on the form. If your request is denied or you never filed one, your exit date stands, and staff will help you plan your next step: referrals to other programs, transitional options, and continued peer support. 6. Appeals If your extension is denied, you may appeal in writing to the Chief Executive Officer within 3 days of the decision. The CEO’s decision is final. There is no appeal beyond the CEO. 7. Adoption This policy is issued as CSP-ST-001, dated October 2, 2026. It takes effect upon the opening of the Centipede shelter and only after formal adoption by the Board of Directors. It works alongside the Check-In Form & Stay Agreement (CSP-CI-001) and the Shelter Security Policy (CSP-SEC-001); where they overlap on safety, the Security Policy governs. Levi A.
