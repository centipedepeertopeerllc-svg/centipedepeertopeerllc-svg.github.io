Document No. CPP-POL-2026-020 — Communications Office Policy, DRAFT (number assigned November 12, 2026, for tracking; effective only on adoption) CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC Communications Office Policy **Status: DRAFT — prepared November 10, 2026, at the direction of Levi Allen Spears, CEO. For review at the Friday, November 13, 2026 grand-opening work session and for board review (board packet, November 27, 2026). Not yet in force.** **Company: Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — a California for-profit limited liability company, LIC 0714781** --- 1. What this office is The Communications Office is Centipede's telecommunications office. It exists for one purpose: **program communications with the public about our programs** — how the programs are worked within the community, and communicating with the community about them. It is a **communications office, not a business office.** 2. This policy is standing This policy stands for **whoever contacts the office, for any reason**, and for everyone who works in the office or speaks for it. There are no exceptions by caller, by program, or by reason for the contact. If a request falls outside this policy, the office does not handle it — it is directed to the proper company channel. 3. What the office is used for — program communications only The office is used for program communications only, including: - Giving the public information about Centipede's programs — what each program is and its honest status (Active / In Development / Pending Board Approval). - Answering questions about the company's published work calendar and program schedules. - Communicating with the community about how the programs are worked in the community. - Connecting callers to the right program through the program leads' extensions. - Outreach coordination communication and community-partner communication about programs. 4. What the office is not used for - Business-office functions: no payments, billing, contracting, or business transactions of any kind. - Anything clinical: Centipede provides non-clinical peer support only. The office does not diagnose, counsel, or give medical, psychiatric, or legal advice. - Outcome promises: the office describes the services offered, never results achieved. 5. Non-published office — no address The Communications Office is a **non-published office**. **No address for this office is published or used publicly for any purpose** — not on the website, not on social media, not in directories, not in documents. It is strictly a work office for its designated purpose, not a public walk-in location. 6. The phone rule - The **office phone number is the only number listed for this office.** No other number — including any personal or business cell number — is published or given out as a number for this office. - The office has **its own extensions for its program leads**. Callers reach a program through the office number and that program lead's extension. (Extensions are assigned to program leads as the office is set up; assignments are recorded with this policy when made.) - Emergency calls to the office are handled by urgency, under the company's standing practice. In an emergency, call 911 first. 7. Confidentiality stands in this office The company's HIPAA-level confidentiality law applies in this office in full. **No client information is given out through this office to anyone.** Client information is disclosed only under the company's standing rule: a signed Release of Information from the client, or a judge-signed court order — a subpoena alone is not a court order. Statistics, if ever given, are aggregates only, never traceable to one person. 8. Honest program communication Every communication from this office follows the company's public standards: honest program status labels, non-clinical language, no outcome promises, and the company stated plainly as a California for-profit limited liability company. Nothing is presented as operating that is in development or pending approval. 9. Changes to the office's use The office is used for what is set out in this policy — nothing else. **If the company decides to use the office for anything more, that use is first written into the company guidelines, standards, and policies.** Until it is written there, it is not a use of this office. 10. Review This policy is reviewed at the November 13, 2026 grand-opening work session, presented for board review, and reviewed again whenever the office's use changes under Section 9. --- *Drafted by Jarvis, Board Officer, November 10, 2026, from Levi's directions of the same evening: communications office, not a business office; program communications only with the public about our programs; a telecommunications office; non-published, no address; office number the only number listed, with extensions for program leads; standing for whoever contacts the office for any reason; added uses written into company guidelines, standards, and policies first.*
