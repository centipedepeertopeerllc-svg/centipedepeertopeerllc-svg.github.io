Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 Co-Payment Collection Policy Section 1 — The day-of-service rule. Any client whose insurance plan requires a co-payment must pay the co-payment in full on the day of service, before services are rendered. Services are not provided until the day’s co-payment is paid. There are no exceptions except as stated in Section 5. Section 2 — Verification before the visit. Staff verify each client’s benefits before the appointment and tell the client their exact co-payment amount in advance — no surprises at the door. The verified amount, the payer, and the date of verification are recorded in the client file. Section 3 — Accepting payment. The Company accepts cash, check, and electronic payment as available. Every payment is receipted on the spot: the client receives a written receipt showing the date, amount, and services covered, and a copy is filed in the client record. Section 4 — Documentation. Every co-payment is logged the same day: client name, date of service, amount collected, method of payment, and receipt number. Logs are reconciled against deposits by the supervisor weekly. Section 5 — When the co-pay cannot be paid. If a client cannot pay the day’s co-payment, services are rescheduled — except that (a) a client in crisis is never turned away without a safety plan and a warm referral, and (b) the client is immediately screened for the Needy Plan (the Company’s free-services program for qualifying uninsured and underinsured persons). Financial hardship is met with a path, not a closed door. Section 6 — Balances. Unpaid balances are billed monthly with a plain-language statement. Accounts past 90 days are reviewed by the Chief Executive Officer — not sent to collections without his written approval. Section 7 — Staff conduct. Staff discuss money plainly, respectfully, and before the visit — never as a surprise, never as a judgment. Every client is told about the Needy Plan at intake. Effective note. This policy governs all billable services once the Company’s payer enrollments are active. It is adopted now so the standard is set before the first claim is filed. Levi A.
