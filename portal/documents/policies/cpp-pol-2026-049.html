CPP-POL-2026-049 — Mandated Reporter Seal Use on Company Letterhead

CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-049 — Mandated Reporter Seal Use on Company Letterhead Issued November 18, 2026, by direction of the Chief Executive Officer. 1. PURPOSE. The company's Mandated Reporter certification (CPP-POL-2026-048) marks the people Centipede has trained and certified to carry the reporting duty for children, elders, and dependent adults. A certification that cannot be seen does only half its work. This policy makes the certification visible on the company's own paper: every certified person uses the Mandated Reporter seal on company letterhead, alongside his or her own seal, so that anyone reading a Centipede letter can see that its writer stands certified under the company's mandated reporter program. The rule is mandatory and system-wide, with no exceptions. 2. THE RULE. Every person in the company certified under the Mandated Reporter certification established by CPP-POL-2026-048 must place the Mandated Reporter seal on anything he or she writes on company letterhead, alongside his or her own seal. The seal goes on every letterhead document the certified person authors — letters, notices, memoranda, and every other document written on the company's letterhead — in every program, from the first document written after certification. There are no exceptions: no program, no document type, and no circumstance excuses the seal. 3. THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER'S LETTERHEADS. The Chief Executive Officer is certified under the certification (certificate CPP-MR-2026-001) and is the program's trainer. His letterheads carry the Mandated Reporter seal alongside the CEO seal, on the same terms as every other certified person's letterhead. The rule binds the top of the company first. 4. PLACEMENT. The Mandated Reporter seal stands in the seal row at the close of the document, alongside the writer's own seal and the company's seals, at the same size and in the same manner as the company's other seals. It is placed when the document is written, not added afterward. A certified person's letterhead document is not finished, and does not go out, until the seal is on it. 5. WHO MAY USE THE SEAL. The Mandated Reporter seal is used only by a person certified under CPP-POL-2026-048, and only from the day the certificate issues. It is never used by a person whose certificate has not yet issued or is held pending completion, and never on a document the writer did not author. The seal certifies the writer; it does not certify the document, and it is not a decoration. 6. RELATION TO THE CERTIFICATE. The seal and the certificate stand together. Under CPP-POL-2026-048 and the Mandated Reporter certificate register, no certificate is given unless it carries both the Mandated Reporter seal and the signature of Levi Allen Spears as Trainer. This policy is the other half of that rule: the seal that stands on a person's certificate follows that person onto the company's letterhead for as long as he or she writes under the certification. 7. RECORDS AND CORRECTIONS. The Record Keeper keeps the Mandated Reporter certificate register current; the register is the company's record of who is certified, and therefore of who carries this duty. A letterhead document found to have gone out from a certified writer without the Mandated Reporter seal is corrected and reissued, and the correction is noted to the Record Keeper the same day it is found. Repeated omission is addressed under the company's personnel rules. 8. SCOPE AND MONEY. This policy authorizes no purchase; the Mandated Reporter seal already exists as a company seal, made November 18, 2026. Nothing in this policy states or implies nonprofit or charitable status: Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company. This policy works alongside CPP-POL-2026-048, whose Section 16 states the same requirement inside the Mandated Reporter Policy; where the two speak, they speak as one rule. Board notification of this policy is set for the board meeting of November 27, 2026.
