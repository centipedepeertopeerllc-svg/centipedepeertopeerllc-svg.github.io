Document No. CPP-POL-2026-004 — Program Funding Policy (number assigned November 12, 2026, under the CPP-POL numbering standard) PROGRAM FUNDING POLICY Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — LIC 0714781 Issued by Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer — November 11, 2026 Filed in the packet for the Board of Directors meeting of November 27, 2026. This is one policy. It has one set of rules for every program, and one schedule per program. PART ONE — THE RULES. THEY APPLY TO EVERY PROGRAM. NO EXCEPTIONS. Rule 1. Two approvals before money moves. The Chief Executive Officer approves first. The program director approves second, because the director holds the program's funds. No approval, no spending. Rule 2. Approval tiers. Up to $100: the CEO's word, logged the same day. $100 to $500: written approval from both approvers. Over $500: a signed purchase order before anything is bought. An unsigned purchase order buys nothing. Zero exceptions. Rule 3. A card is not permission. Holding a program card authorizes nothing. Card issuance is readiness, not spending authority. Spending authority comes only from Rules 1 and 2. Rule 4. Restricted money stays restricted. Money given for a program is spent on that program only. The trail is documented at every step: source, intake, separation into the program's ledger, spend. Example on record: the $1,500 SJC HRCS program check for homeless outreach, received November 11, 2026, deposited to Chase, then separated into Mercury for the Outreach program. Rule 5. Money follows the work, not the card. Peer support is funded against documented peer hours and documented outcomes, never against card volume. Referral and ID money is spent on direct costs only, with receipts, because those dollars carry the cleanest trail in the company. Rule 6. Same-day record. Every spend is entered in the Centipede Banking Program the day it happens, with its tax category set in the Tax Center. A receipt is kept for every spend of $75 or more, and for any spend a funder could ask about, at any amount. Rule 7. No funding source, no spending. A new initiative spends nothing until its funding source is identified in writing and the Board has approved it. Programs that are not cleared to operate do not spend. See Part Two. Rule 8. One card per program. Each program's money rides on its own card and its own ledger. Program funds are never mixed, never borrowed across programs, and never moved between programs without both approvals under Rule 1, recorded in writing. Rule 9. Named-holder cards. A card issued to a named person — the field team card, the assistant card — is spent by that person only, on that role's work only. The payroll card is payroll only. It is not a program card. PART TWO — THE SCHEDULES. ONE PER PROGRAM. Schedule A. Homeless Outreach (Active). Card: Outreach. Operating policy in effect. Funds are tied to verified program funding — checks and grants verified at receipt — and follow the Rule 4 trail from receipt to service. Spends: outreach supplies, hygiene kits, water, fuel, and direct field costs. Schedule B. 1-on-1 Peer Support (Active). Card: General program funding as assigned per case. Funded under Rule 5: documented peer hours and outcomes. The two-step approval of Rule 1 is the standing process for client funding requests, as documented in client cases since November 3, 2026. Schedule C. Referrals & ID Assistance, and Referrals & Hygiene (Active). Cards: Referrals & IDs; Referrals & Hygiene. Direct costs only: government fees, document costs, hygiene supplies. Receipt for every spend, any amount. Schedule D. Substance Abuse Recovery Support (Adopted; not yet enrolling). Card: Substance Abuse. Spending: ZERO on services until the program opens for enrollment. Planning spends only, each one approved in advance by the CEO in writing. Schedule E. Justice Support (Draft; pending counsel review). Card: Justice Support. Spending: ZERO until counsel clears the program's guardrails and the program's status changes in writing. Schedule F. Veterans Program (Pending board approval). Card: Veterans. Spending: ZERO until the Board approves the program. Schedule G. Shelter (In development). Card: Shelter. Development spends only, each approved in advance by the CEO in writing and reported to the Board. No operating spends until a site and the funding entity path are settled and the program's status changes in writing. Schedule H. General (Company overhead). Card: General. CEO only. Company operating costs that belong to no single program. Schedule I. Super Hero Program (In Development — Pending Board Approval Nov 27, 2026). Card: Super Hero Program — NOT YET SET UP. Set-up of the card is deferred at the CEO's direction. Spending: ZERO until the Board adopts the program AND the CEO directs set-up of its own program card in writing. The program will have its own program card and its own funding, kept separate from every other program under Rule 8 — no commingling, no borrowing across programs. After set-up, it operates under Part One (the spine) and the same card rules as every other program card: its own per-card ledger with a running balance, monthly statements per card with statement document numbers, corrections by reversing entry only, Mercury named in plain text only, no full card number, CVC, or expiration date held — masked to the last four digits only, Mercury-synced transactions held in Pending Review and assigned by the CEO himself (nothing posts to the card on its own), receipts at $75 and over, and every spend entered the same day with its tax category. The card is spent only by the person named to hold it, on Super Hero Program purchases only. No public cash solicitation for this program until licensed California counsel clears the charitable-solicitation requirements. The card's daily limit is to be confirmed with Mercury at set-up (Open Item 4). OPEN ITEMS CARRIED WITH THIS POLICY 1. The limits for the assistant card and the General card were not stated in those notices and are to be confirmed with Mercury. 2. Purchase orders PO-002 through PO-005 remain unsigned. Under Rule 2 they authorize nothing until signed. 3. The funding entity path (fiscal sponsorship and/or the nonprofit affiliate) remains an open company decision; grant funding that cannot flow through the LLC follows that decision, not this policy. 4. The Super Hero Program card (Schedule I) does not exist yet and is not set up.
