This Policy establishes the Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC Hygiene Outreach Program — the organized distribution of hygiene kits and related personal-care supplies to individuals experiencing homelessness and housing instability in Stockton and San Joaquin County, as part of the Company's peer- support and homeless-outreach mission. 1. Program Description The Hygiene Outreach Program provides sealed hygiene kits containing essential personal-care items to individuals encountered during Centipede's street, camp, and community outreach. Hygiene kits are distributed as a dignity-first, no-strings-attached act of care, and as a bridge to deeper engagement: a clean kit opens the door to a conversation, and a conversation opens the door to peer support. 2. Kit Contents Each standard hygiene kit shall contain, at minimum, the items listed in the Company's Hygiene Kit Contents Letter (CENTIPEDE-HYG-2026-001A). The Chief Executive Officer may approve seasonal or situational additions (e.g., sunscreen in summer, hand warmers in winter) and specialty kits (e.g., feminine hygiene kits). 3. Distribution Standards • Kits are distributed free of charge. No individual is ever asked to pay, trade, or provide anything in exchange for a kit. • Kits are distributed without conditions — no sobriety requirement, no intake form, no identification required. • Distribution is paired with a peer-support offer: every kit handoff includes a genuine invitation to talk, connect, or accept further support. The invitation may be declined without affecting the kit. • Outreach workers shall respect declined offers and never pressure, follow, or photograph recipients. • Workers shall follow all field safety and sanitation practices in the Centipede Field Training Manual. 4. Sourcing and Inventory Kit supplies are sourced through donations, grants, partnerships, and Company funds as available. The Company maintains a simple inventory log of kits assembled and distributed. Donated supplies are used as intended by the donor unless the donor directs otherwise. 5. Record-Keeping The Company records the number of kits distributed by date and general outreach location. No recipient names, identifying information, or photographs are recorded as a condition of receiving a kit. Aggregate counts support grant reporting and program evaluation only. 6. Non-Discrimination Kits are distributed without regard to race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic. 7. Effective Date and Review This Policy takes effect upon approval by the Chief Executive Officer and remains in effect until amended. It shall be reviewed at least annually. Levi A.
