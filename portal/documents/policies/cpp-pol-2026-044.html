CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC CPP-POL-2026-044 — Program Binders and Dashboard Records Policy DRAFT — November 18, 2026 — for the Chief Executive Officer's approval; board notification November 27, 2026 1. A BINDER FOR EVERY PROGRAM. Every program of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC has its own program binder in the company Library (Program Binders/<program>). If a program has no binder, one is opened the same day the program is created or the gap is found. The master index of program binders is kept at the front of the Program Binders folder. 2. THE CONNECTION. Each program binder names its program's dashboard in the Company Portal, its governing policy, its program status, and its Google Drive folder. Each program dashboard names its binder on its face. Either one leads to the other; neither stands alone. 3. FILING. Every document a dashboard produces for a program is filed in that program's binder under the same document number the dashboard assigned it, and entered in the binder's document register the same day. The binders are the official record; the dashboards are the working surface (CPP-POL-2026-031). 4. CLIENT FILES. Client case material is never filed in a program binder. It lives in the client's own locked binder under the standing client-records rule, and intake records follow CPP-POL-2026-043. 5. EVERYTHING CARRIES A POLICY. Every program, every dashboard, and every standing company system operates under a written policy in the company register. When something new is built — a program, a dashboard, a system, a standing practice — its policy is drafted in the same work session, filed in the Policies binder and the Legal Binder, and listed for board notification. A gap found in the policy register is closed the day it is found and the closing is recorded. 6. MONEY. This policy authorizes no purchase. Program purchases follow the purchase-order process (CPP-POL-2026-028 and CPP-POL-2026-029). Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company; nothing in binder or dashboard materials may state or imply nonprofit or charitable status. Prepared by Jarvis, Board Officer — Record Keeper, November 18, 2026, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer.
