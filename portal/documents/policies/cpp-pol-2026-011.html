Purpose This policy explains how Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC ("Centipede," "we," "our") handles information relating to the people who work for us, and what privacy our team members can expect in the course of their work activities. It applies to all employees, trainees, contractors, and volunteers (collectively, "team members"). 2. Work Activities Covered Centipede's work includes direct homeless outreach in the community, peer support, nonclinical mental-health outreach, substance-use stabilization and treatment referrals, warm referrals to shelter and housing, follow-up coordination, documentation of services, and coordination with partners such as the Stockton Police Department and the San Joaquin County Probation Department. This policy covers all work activities performed in these roles, including: • Field outreach shifts, travel between outreach sites, and on-call availability; • Communications made for work purposes, including phone calls, text messages, email, and messaging apps on company accounts or devices; • Documentation of outreach encounters, referrals, and follow-up in company records and forms; • Use of company vehicles, equipment, phones, computers, and software systems; • Timekeeping, attendance reporting, mileage logs, and expense reporting; and • Training, supervision meetings, and performance evaluations. 3. Company Systems: No Expectation of Personal Privacy All company-provided systems and accounts — including company email addresses, phone numbers, devices, computers, software, cloud storage, and social media accounts operated for Centipede — are company property provided for work purposes. Team members should have no expectation of personal privacy in any information created, sent, received, or stored on company systems. Centipede may access, review, monitor, and disclose such information at any time, with or without notice, for legitimate business purposes including supervision, quality assurance, safety, and legal compliance. 4. Monitoring of Work Activities To support safety, accountability, and quality of services, Centipede may monitor work activities, which may include: • Review of work-related communications and documentation for completeness, accuracy, and professionalism; • Location information from company devices or vehicles during scheduled work hours, used for team safety and coordination during field outreach; • Timekeeping and attendance records; and • Photographs or video from outreach activities taken in accordance with our photo authorization practices. Monitoring is limited to work activities and work systems. Centipede does not monitor team members' purely personal communications, personal devices, or personal accounts, except where required by law or where a personal device has been voluntarily enrolled for specific work functions with the team member's written consent. Information We Collect About Team Members We collect information needed to employ, pay, supervise, and support our team, which may include: • Contact and identification information (name, address, phone, email, emergency contacts); • Employment eligibility, payroll, tax, and benefits information; • Qualifications, training records, certifications, and performance documentation; • Background check results where required for the role and permitted by law; and • Work product described in Section 2, created in the course of assigned duties. 6. How We Use and Share Team Information Team information is used for employment administration, payroll and benefits, supervision and training, safety, and compliance with legal obligations. We share it only as needed: with payroll and benefits providers, with government agencies as required by law, and with partner agencies when coordination of services requires it and appropriate authorizations are in place. We do not sell team members' personal information. 7. Confidentiality Obligations of Team Members Our work brings team members into contact with sensitive information about the people we serve. Every team member must safeguard client and company confidential information, use it only as needed to perform assigned duties, and never disclose it except as authorized by a signed release or as required by law. These obligations survive the end of the working relationship. Nothing in this policy limits a team member's rights under applicable labor or whistleblower protection laws. Client privacy: Information about the people we serve is additionally protected by our HIPAA authorization practices, release-of-information procedures, and photo authorization requirements. Team members must complete required privacy training before beginning field work. 8. Access, Retention, and Questions Team members may request to review their own personnel records as provided by law by contacting the Chief Executive Officer. Work records are retained as needed for business, legal, and funding-accountability purposes. Questions about this policy should be directed to the Chief Executive Officer. 9. Changes to This Policy Centipede may update this policy as our operations or legal requirements change. Team members will be notified of material changes, and continued work following notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Approved by: Finalized: October 9, 2026 at 12:00 PM PDT Levi A.
