CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC HUMAN RESOURCES DEPARTMENT — CHARTER Charter No. CHR-2026-002 Established November 13, 2026, by direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer 1. ESTABLISHMENT The Human Resources Department of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is established effective November 13, 2026, by order of the Chief Executive Officer. It stands alongside the Legal Department (Charter CHR-2026-001) as an internal department of the company. 2. PURPOSE The HR Department is the company's home for its people: hiring and onboarding, personnel records, training records, workplace policies that concern employees and volunteers, and the orderly documentation of every person's role, permissions, and standing in the company. 3. SCOPE The HR Department is responsible for: - Personnel files: one file per employee/volunteer, holding role description, start date, permissions granted, and training completed. - Onboarding: the standard steps every new person completes (identity and contact record, role and permissions letter, required policies read and acknowledged, training scheduled). The 90-day graduated-permissions practice for new access is administered here. - Authorization records: who is authorized to use company systems and assistants, in what scope (see CPP-POL-2026-001 §4.5), with effective dates. - Training: scheduling and recording required training (including IT-schedule sessions such as voiceprint and system authorization setup). - Employee/volunteer policies: maintaining the personnel-facing policies (Employee Use CPP-POL-2026-003 draft; Meal & Rest Break CPP-POL-2026-007 draft; Employee Privacy CPP-POL-2026-011) and carrying drafts to the CEO and board for approval. - Offboarding and standing changes: recording departures, terminations of access, and changes of role, the day they happen. - Payroll coordination records: who is on payroll and their card assignments in the Centipede Banking Program (assignment of transactions remains the CEO's alone under the manual-assignment rule; HR keeps the people-side record only — HR does not move money). 4. LIMITS - The HR Department is an internal administrative function. It gives no legal advice; employment-law questions go to licensed outside counsel through the Legal Department's counsel-review queue. - HR records are confidential — CEO and authorized officers only. Personnel information is disclosed only on the same standard as all company confidential records. - Nothing in this charter changes the company's governance: the CEO holds final authority; the board's role is unchanged. 5. ADMINISTRATION Until the CEO names an HR head, the department is administered by Jarvis, Board Officer and Record Keeper, under the CEO's direction, and its records live in this department's binder in the company Library, cross-filed to the Legal Binder where a matter is legal in nature (per the one-binder rule, legal items file into CPP-BND-2026-001 the same session). 6. ADDENDUM — November 13, 2026 (CEO direction, same day) - The HR Dashboard runs on the company's Family Run Company Standard (adopted Nov 13, 2026) and follows the Client Dashboard pattern: two separate areas — an administrator area and a member area where each employee, volunteer, or executive administrative member sees only their own record, their hours, and what HR holds on them. The dashboard is on lockdown: personal private information, no leaks. - Website: the dashboard is also to be put on the company website through the secure Workbench Portal sign-in front door (per-person sign-in, Centipede people only), with the same security measures enforced there in full — no weaker website version, and no HR data on any public page. - Front of the dashboard carries the company logo and its name: Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — Human Resources Department. - Feature pattern modeled on best-practice HR software (BambooHR people-management model; Gusto all-in-one model), researched Nov 13, 2026: central personnel records with document storage, onboarding checklists, time and attendance, time-off approvals, self-service, org chart, role-based access, audit trail, HR reporting. Payroll money movement stays in the Centipede Banking Program under the CEO's manual-assignment rule. ID numbers NEVER start with the digit 1. - ID VERIFIES IDENTITY (CEO clarification, Nov 13, 2026): the employee ID is an identity check, not just a label — clocking in or out requires the person's own ID together with their own authenticated sign-in, so the system verifies the entry belongs to that person. No clocking for another person. - REGISTER OPENED Nov 13, 2026 (employee-id-register-2026-11-13.txt): 2001 CEO Levi Allen Spears; 2002 Junior (Field Team); 2003 Monica Frishkorn-Wellner (Board Director); 2004 Jeremy Hughes (Board Member).
