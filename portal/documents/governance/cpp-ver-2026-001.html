Filed in the Centipede Legal Department Binder, CPP-BND-2026-001, Tab 4 — Verifications. 1. What this verification is — and what it is not This document is a verification of the company's own records. At the direction of the Chief Executive Officer, the records listed in Section 2 were examined to verify, from the record itself, the basis on which Jarvis has been used by Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC from the start of that use through November 12, 2026: who directed the use, under what written policy, with what controls, and what remains open. **This verification is not a legal opinion.** It states what the company's records show. Final legal judgment on any question touched on here rests with the company's licensed counsel, and the open items in Section 4 are expressly reserved to counsel. 2. Records examined - CPP-POL-2026-001, Policy on Jarvis — Role, Use, Authority, and Separation of Records (approved November 12, 2026), with Memorandum M-1, Proof of Authority Controls, and its exhibits. - The Secretary of State exchange: the company's outgoing package of November 11, 2026 (company Gmail thread 1a1cdcd1d2b1b421) and the reply of George Garrett, Supervising Program Technician II, California Secretary of State, Business Programs Division, November 12, 2026 — filed at Tab 3 of this binder. - The CPP-POL Policy Document Numbering Standard (adopted November 12, 2026) and the Master Document Register, including the Company Policies section (CPP-POL-2026-001 through CPP-POL-2026-021). - This binder itself, CPP-BND-2026-001, and the dated standing records cited in Memorandum M-1. 3. Nothing in the records examined shows Jarvis acting on the direction of any other person. **3.2 A written policy framework, adopted on counsel's advice.** CPP-POL-2026-001 was adopted November 12, 2026 at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer on the advice of the company's business attorney — that anyone can come to the app and demand things of Jarvis without the CEO's knowledge, and the authority situation should be answered in writing. The policy is published on the company's public website under "Meet Jarvis," together with Memorandum M-1, so the answer is open to anyone, including for legal purposes. The companion drafts CPP-POL-2026-002 (Community Use) and CPP-POL-2026-003 (Employee Use) are marked DRAFT and are not in effect. **3.3 Controls in operation, dated, with evidence.** Memorandum M-1 records, and its exhibits evidence, the controls under which the work has been done: verify before acting and read-back of every send against the approved text (standing since November 8, 2026 — Exhibit A records the control catching two sends whose stored bodies carried matter that was not in the approved text); the client-records disclosure law (November 10, 2026 — disclosure only on a client's signed Release of Information or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone does not suffice); the no-deletion rule (November 10, 2026), with the same day's deletion incident and the restore of the client binders accounted 115 of 115 against the Master Document Register; banking access read-only through Plaid, with no full card numbers, CVCs, or expiration dates held anywhere in company systems; the CEO's personal inbox read-only and never sent from; and the company spending hold in force. **3.4 The Secretary of State question was asked and answered in writing.** On November 11, 2026 the company sent its bylaws, operating agreement, and partnership agreement to the California Secretary of State and asked, in writing, whether its formation and licensing were in order. The reply of November 12, 2026 states that bylaws and operating agreements are maintained by the entity and are not filed with the Secretary of State, and that the Secretary of State does not handle licensing. The record shows the company asked rather than assumed, and made no representation of a filing or license that the record does not support. **3.5 A records system that carries the proof.** Every company policy carries a number under the CPP-POL Numbering Standard (CPP-POL-2026-001 through CPP-POL-2026-021; numbers are never reused), is entered in the Master Document Register, and is filed in this binder. Company records are kept daily; the platform-restart record alone stands at 195 filings logged through November 12, 2026. **3.6 Incidents are in the record, not hidden.** The records examined include the company's own failures and their corrections — the November 8, 2026 send defect caught by read-back, and the November 10, 2026 deletion incident and full restore. This verification treats those recorded corrections as evidence that the controls operate, and reports them rather than omitting them. 4.
