The Series Every policy of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC carries a professional document number in the CPP-POL series: CPP-POL-YYYY-NNN — CPP (Centipede Peer to Peer), POL (policy), YYYY (the year the series entry was opened), NNN (a three-digit sequence number, assigned once and never reused). The series was opened on November 12, 2026, at the direction of the Chief Executive Officer, beginning with the Policy on Jarvis as CPP-POL-2026-001, and extended the same day to every existing company policy in a system-wide sweep, so that no policy of this company stands without a number. 2. Rules • One number per policy. A policy keeps its number through every revision and restatement. A superseded version carries the same number, marked SUPERSEDED, with the date it was superseded. • Numbers are never reused. A retired policy's number stays retired with it. • New policies are numbered at approval, in the next open sequence for the year, before they are distributed. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC | Policy Document Numbering Standard — CPP-POL Series — Adopted November 12, 2026 | Page 1 of 4 • Drafts prepared for approval carry their assigned number with the status DRAFT plainly marked; the number takes full effect on approval. A draft that is never adopted keeps its number in the register, marked NOT ADOPTED, so the sequence has no silent gaps. • Where the number appears. On the face of the policy itself (title block, and the footer where the build carries one), in the Master Document Register's policy section, and in this standard's assignment table. Copies already distributed before a number was assigned are not recalled; the number attaches to the company's record copy and to every later printing. • This standard is kept with the policies it governs. Amendments only by the Chief Executive Officer, in writing. 3.
