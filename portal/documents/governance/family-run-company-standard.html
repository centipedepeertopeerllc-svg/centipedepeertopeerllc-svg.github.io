CENTIPEDE PEER TO PEER, LLC — FAMILY RUN COMPANY STANDARD Adopted by direction of Levi Allen Spears, Chief Executive Officer — November 13, 2026 Company motto: "Family Run Company." WHAT "FAMILY RUN" MEANS FOR CENTIPEDE — LOCKED IN Researched November 13, 2026 from established family-business practice, and adopted by the CEO as Centipede's standing definition. A family-run company, as this company practices it: 1. OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL STAY IN THE FAMILY. The company is owned and directed by the family. Final decisions rest with the family head — the CEO — and are not handed to strangers. 2. FAMILY RUNS FIRST. Family comes before outside interests in how the company is run. The people leading the company know each other, answer to each other, and sit at the same table after decisions are made. 3. LONG-TERM OVER SHORT-TERM. The company is built to last and to be handed on — decisions are made for the legacy, not for a quick gain. 4. THE FOUNDER'S VALUES ARE THE CULTURE. Honesty, keeping your word, and treating people right are not slogans; they are how the work is done every day, set by the founder and carried by everyone who joins. 5. THE FAMILY NAME STANDS BEHIND THE WORK. When Centipede gives its word, the family's name and reputation guarantee it. That is why the record is kept clean and every document is done properly. 6. DIRECT COMMUNICATION, FAST DECISIONS. Fewer layers between people means things are said plainly and decided quickly — and disagreements are handled inside the family, on the record, and settled. 7. PEOPLE ARE NOT NUMBERS. Family-run means the people the company serves are treated the way the family would want its own treated. Nobody walks alone. 8. STEWARDSHIP OF THE COMMUNITY. The company sees itself as a steward of its community — its success is measured by what it gives back to the place it lives in. 9. GROWING THE NEXT GENERATION IN. Family members are brought up inside the company — taught the systems, the records, and the standards first, then given more responsibility step by step as they prove ready. Succession is planned, not improvised. 10. FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE. The company spends carefully, keeps clean books, and answers for every dollar — because the money carries the family name too. This standard governs how Centipede presents itself and how its people are taught the company. Locked in November 13, 2026, at the CEO's direction. Recorded by Jarvis, Board Officer and Record Keeper.
