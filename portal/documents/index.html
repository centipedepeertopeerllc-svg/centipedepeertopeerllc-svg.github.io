Company Documents

One place for every company document the team works by — the documents themselves, readable here in the Portal: every policy, the manuals, the forms staff use, the company's governance documents and standards, and every program's dashboard and Drive folder. Forms Staff Use

  • Purchase Order Form (Form CPP-PO) — read the form here. The blank form is filed with the Financial Officer's position papers in the HR Department binder; completed purchase orders are filed in the PO Binder, and client purchase orders in the Client POs binder (Finance). The rules are CPP-POL-2026-028 and CPP-POL-2026-029: no PO, no purchase, and every PO over $250 carries the CEO's countersignature.
  • Centipede Intake Packet — including the Intake & Participation Agreement — read it here. Official filed copy: Forms binder — Centipede Intake Packet. Intake is governed by CPP-POL-2026-043: a copy of the person's government photo ID, a signed Release of Information, and the signed Intake & Participation Agreement come before membership. Never write down an ID number.
  • Release of Information (ROI) Form — read it here. Official filed copy: Forms binder — Centipede Release of Information. Client information is released only on a signed ROI or a judge-signed court order; a subpoena alone is not a court order.
  • DAP Note Skeleton (DAP-SKEL-01) — the company's standard shape for session notes: Data, Assessment, Plan. Official filed copy: DAP Note Skeleton binder. Notes themselves are written in the DAP Notes Dashboard, the same day as the work.
  • SB 294 — Workplace Know Your Rights Notice & Emergency Contact Designation Form — the current employee notice under California's Workplace Know Your Rights Act (SB 294, Labor Code §§ 1550–1559), provided to every employee at hire and annually on or before February 1. Read the full notice and the emergency-contact designation form here. The Spanish version is filed in the employee packets and the company Drive; official filed copies: HR Department binder (sb-294-notice/) and Legal Binder, Tab 5.
