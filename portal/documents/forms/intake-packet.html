Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207 CalMHSA Driven Intake Packet — Before Membership Policy CPP-POL-2026-043 · Worked in the Client Dashboard (Company Portal) · November 18, 2026 What intake is Intake comes first. No one becomes a member of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, or of any of its programs, until an intake for that person has been completed and approved. Intake is non-clinical peer support: we ask, we listen, we record what the person tells us, and we mark gaps plainly. A blank is an honest answer, never a guess. How to work an intake — step by step • 1. Open the intake. In the Client Dashboard, go to "Intake — before membership" and fill in the form as you talk with the person. Ask what applies; leave the rest blank. • 2. Work the documents. In the intake register, mark each document as it stands — Not yet, Received, Self-declared, or Does not apply. Copies of documents go in the person's binder, never in Google Drive, never by email. • 3. Approve when it is ready. The Approve button unlocks when the three required documents are in (below). Approving creates the person's member record — the dashboard assigns the next client code (CP-####) and client number, and the person appears in the Clients list for encounters, referrals, and enrollments. • 4. Carry it into the binder. Tell Jarvis the intake number (INT-####) and he carries the intake into the member's locked binder, which is the official record. • 5. If it is not approved, mark it so — and still offer referrals and resource navigation. Intake decides membership, not whether someone is treated with respect. The standing rules • No identification numbers, ever. No Social Security number, no ID card number, no Veteran ID Card number is asked for, spoken, or recorded — on the form, in notes, or anywhere else. • Confidentiality. Intake information is client information: disclosed only on a signed client release of information or a judge-signed court order. • Non-clinical language. We support, encourage, coach, mentor, navigate, connect to resources, share lived experience, facilitate groups, and advocate. We do not diagnose, treat, or counsel clinically. In an emergency, call 911. • Veteran status is the person's to share — asked once, recorded as they answer, never published.
