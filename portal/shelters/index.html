San Joaquin County Shelter & Housing Directory

Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC — a California for-profit limited liability company · 717 E. Longview Ave., Stockton, CA 95207

Community resource directory maintained by Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC for peer support and resource navigation. Listings are other organizations' programs — call ahead to confirm beds, intake hours, and eligibility. In an emergency call 911. For live shelter matching, dial 2-1-1 or text your ZIP to 898211.

Current as of November 18, 2026. A listing here is information only — it does not mean Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC partners with, speaks for, or guarantees a bed at any organization listed.

Start Here — 2-1-1 San Joaquin

211 San Joaquin — free, confidential, 24 hours

Phone: Dial 2-1-1 or (800) 436-9997 · Text your ZIP code to 898211 · Online: 211sj.org

Serves: Everyone in San Joaquin County.

Note: The live route for shelter matching — 211 holds current bed and program information across the county, including cities with no shelter of their own (Escalon, Ripon, Lathrop).

  • Women with children: Gospel Center New Hope Family Shelter; Haven of Peace; Lodi House; HOPE Raymus House (Manteca)
  • Families with children: Stockton Shelter Family Shelter; Family Promise of San Joaquin County; St. Mary's Family Lodge; Hope Harbor (Lodi); HOPE Family Shelter (Manteca); McHenry House (Tracy); Tracy Temporary Emergency Housing Facility
  • Youth (ages 12–24): PREVAIL Youth Services (Stockton); St. Mary's TAY beds
  • Fleeing domestic violence: PREVAIL — DAWN House (Stockton, 24/7 crisis line); Chest of Hope (Tracy)
  • Recovery housing: Gospel Center New Life Program; Salvation Army Adult Rehabilitation Center (Stockton); Emerson House (Tracy)
  • Seniors: Silvercrest Residence (Stockton) — senior housing, not an emergency shelter
  • Housing programs & county doors: Central Valley Low Income Housing Corporation; Housing Authority of the County of San Joaquin; San Joaquin County Human Services Agency
  • Day services & meals: St. Mary's Community Services (Stockton); Stockton Shelter Drop-In Center; Tracy Interfaith Ministries (food & clothing)
  • Seasonal overnight warming centers: City of Stockton Warming Centers (only when the City activates them)

Stockton

Stockton Shelter for the Homeless — Family Shelter & Single Women's Dorm

Address: 411 S. Harrison St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 465-3612

Serves: Families with children; single women.

Note: The shelter's family shelter has 25 rooms and also shelters single women. The shelter runs three year-round shelters and states that when a shelter is full, staff work to connect people with other services or another shelter. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Stockton Shelter for the Homeless — Single Men's Shelter

Address: 611 W. Church St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 466-2605

Serves: Single men.

Note: Run by Stockton Shelter for the Homeless; about 150 bunks, with mats and blankets put out for overflow. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Stockton Shelter for the Homeless — Daytime Drop-In Center

Address: 411 S. Harrison St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 465-3612

Serves: Everyone — day services, no overnight stay.

Note: Open seven days a week, 365 days a year, as a safe daytime place and a connection point to services and referrals. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Gospel Center Rescue Mission — Emergency Lodging (Men)

Address: Main campus: 445 S. San Joaquin St., Stockton, CA 95203 · Men's lodge: 229 E. Church St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 466-2138 · Men's lodge direct: (209) 320-2324

Serves: Men 18 and older experiencing homelessness; men with children are a case-by-case conversation.

Note: Emergency lodging at the oldest operating low-barrier shelter in San Joaquin County. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Gospel Center Rescue Mission — New Hope Family Shelter (Women & Children)

Address: 224 E. Sonora St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 466-2138

Serves: Women 18 and older, with or without children; boys must be under the age of 12.

Note: Call to confirm intake and availability.

Gospel Center Rescue Mission — New Life Program (Residential Recovery)

Address: 445 S. San Joaquin St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: (209) 466-2138

Serves: Men, women, and families.

Note: A 6, 12, or 24-month residential recovery program with life-skills development and job training, leading toward permanent housing. This is Gospel Center's own program description; Centipede's peer support is non-clinical and separate. Call to confirm intake and availability.

St. Mary's Community Services — Lodges & Day Services

Address: 545 W. Sonora St., Stockton, CA 95203

Phone: Main: (209) 467-0703 · Reserve a bed: (209) 636-0651

Serves: Men (Men's Lodge); women (Women's Lodge); families (Family Lodge); transition-age youth (TAY beds); meals and day services for everyone.

Note: St. Mary's posts current bed availability on its own website (smcares.org) — check there, and call the reserve-a-bed line to hold a bed. Three hot meals a day, showers, clothing, and an on-site medical clinic are part of the same campus. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Family Promise of San Joaquin County — Family Shelter & Day Center

Address: 6148 Gettysburg Place, Stockton, CA 95207

Phone: (209) 323-4284

Serves: Families experiencing homelessness with a child under 18.

Note: Up to a 90-day stay. Families sleep at rotating congregation host sites and use the day center (open 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, seven days a week) for showers, laundry, and case management. Call to confirm intake and availability.

PREVAIL — Youth Services (Shelter & Drop-In)

Address: 729 N. California St., Stockton, CA 95204

Phone: Youth services: (209) 929-6700 · Youth Crisis: (209) 948-1911

Serves: Youth ages 12–24.

Note: Shelter, drop-in services, and case management for youth. PREVAIL is the county organization formerly known as Women's Center–Youth & Family Services. Call to confirm intake and availability.

PREVAIL — DAWN House (Domestic Violence Emergency Shelter)

Address: Confidential location, Stockton — call the crisis line first

Phone: 24/7 Crisis Line: (209) 465-4878

Serves: Individuals and families fleeing domestic violence, sexual violence, or human trafficking.

Note: Crisis specialists are available 24/7. The shelter address is confidential for safety — always enter through the crisis line.

The Salvation Army — Stockton Adult Rehabilitation Center

Address: 1247 S. Wilson Way, Stockton, CA 95205

Phone: (209) 466-3871

Serves: Adults.

Note: A six-month residential recovery program with housing, food, and work therapy. The Salvation Army lists program intake hours around the clock; call first. This is the Salvation Army's own program description; Centipede's peer support is non-clinical and separate.

The Salvation Army — Silvercrest Residence (Senior Housing)

Address: 123 N. Stanislaus St., Stockton, CA 95202

Phone: (209) 463-4960

Serves: Seniors.

Note: A senior citizens' residence — long-term senior housing, not an emergency shelter. Call for application and waitlist information.

French Camp

Haven of Peace — Women's Shelter

Address: 7070 S. Harlan Rd., French Camp, CA 95231

Phone: (209) 982-0396

Serves: Single women; women with children (boys must be under the age of 8).

Note: Shelters up to 35 women and children. Stays begin at two weeks and can extend up to six months for residents working with their case manager toward self-sufficiency goals. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Lodi

The Salvation Army Lodi Corps — Hope Harbor Shelter & Family Service Center

Address: 622 N. Sacramento St., Lodi, CA 95240

Phone: (209) 367-9560

Serves: Men, women with children, and families.

Note: Emergency shelter and transitional housing, with free walk-in hot meals served daily at 5:00 PM at the shelter. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Lodi House — Shelter & Transitional Housing

Address: 801 S. Washington St., Lodi, CA 95240

Phone: (209) 334-6346

Serves: Women and children.

Note: A 4–6 month shelter program plus transitional apartments for up to 2 years. Intake: apply in person at the office, Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–5:00 PM. Lodi House is not a domestic-violence safe house; its staff can refer to a safe house program on request.

Inner City Action — Lodi Access Center

Address: 710 N. Sacramento St., Lodi, CA 95240

Phone: 1-800-466-7205

Serves: Adults experiencing homelessness.

Note: The City of Lodi's access center, operated by Inner City Action — overnight stays and services on site. Inner City Action also operates Harmony Homes permanent supportive housing in Lodi, which takes referrals. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Manteca

HOPE Ministries — Raymus House (Women & Children)

Address: 520 Union Rd., Manteca, CA 95337

Phone: (209) 824-0658

Serves: Women with children — girls ages 0–18 and boys ages 0–12.

Note: Emergency shelter for up to 60–90 days, with case management through its Project Hope program. Call to confirm intake and availability.

HOPE Ministries — HOPE Family Shelter & Building Hope

Address: 520 Union Rd., Manteca, CA 95337

Phone: (209) 824-0658

Serves: Families — married couples or single parents, and their children.

Note: Emergency shelter for up to 60–90 days. Building Hope transitional apartments prepare residents to move to their own housing within an 18-month period. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Tracy

City of Tracy — Temporary Emergency Housing Facility

Address: 370 W. Arbor Ave., Tracy, CA 95376

Phone: (209) 208-5701

Serves: Individuals and families experiencing homelessness in the Tracy area.

Note: The City of Tracy's low-barrier emergency housing facility, open since November 2022, with on-site case management. Call to confirm intake and availability.

McHenry House Tracy Family Shelter

Address: 739 A St., Tracy, CA 95376

Phone: (209) 835-2328

Serves: Families — single fathers, mothers, and two-parent families; no age limit on male children living with parents.

Note: 48–72 hour emergency housing and referrals, plus an 8–10 week transitional program. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Chest of Hope — Domestic Violence Shelter

Address: Confidential location, Tracy — call first

Phone: (209) 259-5552

Serves: Women with or without children fleeing domestic violence.

Note: The shelter address is confidential for safety — always enter by phone.

The Coalition (Tracy Citizens to Assist the Homeless) — Emerson House & Rochester House

Address: Tracy — the homes do not publish their addresses, for the privacy of the men living there; you get them when you come in

Phone: (209) 814-0758

Serves: Men.

Note: Emerson House is a transitional recovery home for men rebuilding toward independence; Rochester House is a long-term home for older men and men with disabilities. No application fee. Call and talk through which home fits. Both homes are drug- and alcohol-free and do not run detox on site.

Tracy Interfaith Ministries — Food, Clothing & Referrals (Day Services)

Address: 311 W. Grant Line Rd., Tracy, CA 95376

Phone: (209) 836-5424

Serves: Low-income and homeless individuals and families in Tracy.

Note: Food and clothing, emergency utility assistance, and referrals for services it does not provide. Not a shelter — a day-services door. Call to confirm hours and availability.

Housing Programs & County Doors (Countywide)

Central Valley Low Income Housing Corporation (CVLIHC)

Address: 2431 W. March Lane, Suite 350, Stockton, CA 95207

Phone: (209) 472-7200

Serves: Households and families experiencing or at risk of homelessness in San Joaquin County.

Note: Rapid rehousing, homelessness prevention, and permanent supportive housing programs. CVLIHC participates in the county Coordinated Entry system, which assigns housing based on availability. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Housing Authority of the County of San Joaquin (HACSJ)

Address: 2575 Grand Canal Blvd., Suite 100, Stockton, CA 95207

Phone: (209) 460-5000

Serves: Low-income households across San Joaquin County.

Note: Runs the county's public housing and Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waitlists. Waitlist status changes — HACSJ announces openings on its website (hacsj.org). Call to confirm current status.

San Joaquin County Human Services Agency (HSA)

Address: 333 E. Washington St., Stockton, CA 95202

Phone: (209) 468-1000

Serves: County residents — families, seniors, and individuals.

Note: The county door for CalWORKs, CalFresh, Medi-Cal, and Homeless Assistance. CalWORKs Homeless Assistance can help eligible families with temporary shelter costs — eligibility is decided by the county. Call to confirm intake and availability.

Seasonal — Overnight Warming Centers

City of Stockton Warming Centers — Arnold Rue & Stribley

Address: Arnold Rue Community Center, 5758 Lorraine Ave., Stockton, CA 95210 · Stribley Community Center, 1760 E. Sonora St., Stockton, CA 95205

Phone: Arnold Rue: (209) 937-7350 · Stribley: (209) 937-7351

Serves: Anyone without adequate heat or protection from the cold.

Note: Open overnight, 9:00 PM–7:00 AM, only when the City of Stockton activates them during severe cold weather. Warming centers are a warm place to rest overnight — they are not full shelters and do not provide meals or medical care. Check the City's Severe Weather Resources page (stocktonca.gov, Storms) for the current activation status before you go. SJC libraries are daytime warming centers during regular hours.
