    Law library & tools

    The Justice Support Program's reference shelf. These are research and navigation tools — peer support only, never legal advice and never legal representation. When a member needs advice, the answer is their attorney of record or counsel.

    • Courts & forms
      San Joaquin County Superior Court — our court: departments, calendars, local rules, and forms.
      California Courts (courts.ca.gov) — Judicial Council forms (including the CR criminal series), self-help, and record-cleaning (expungement) guidance.
      San Joaquin County (sjgov.org) — Public Defender, District Attorney, and Probation pages.
    • The law itself
      California Legislative Information — the Penal Code, Welfare & Institutions Code, and every California code, official and current.
      Cornell Legal Information Institute (LII) — plain-language legal reference and federal law.
    • Case research
      Google Scholar — Case Law — free published opinions.
      CourtListener (Free Law Project) — free court opinions and dockets.
    • Reentry & record relief
      Root & Rebound — California reentry legal guides, including the Roadmap to Reentry.
      California Courts self-help (above) — petitions to dismiss under Penal Code 1203.4 and Prop 47 / Prop 64 relief.
    • The bookshelf (reference books)
      California Penal Code (official code — the book every case starts from) · Witkin's California Criminal Law (the standard California criminal-law treatise — read at the San Joaquin County Law Library) · The Criminal Law Handbook: Know Your Rights (Nolo — written for non-lawyers; how courts, charges, and pleas actually work) · Roadmap to Reentry (Root & Rebound — California reentry law, free) · California Criminal Law Procedure and Practice (CEB — the practitioner's desk book, via the law library).
      Book purchases go through the purchase-order process like everything else: no PO, no purchase.
