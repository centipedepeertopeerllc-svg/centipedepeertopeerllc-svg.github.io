JUSTICE SUPPORT MEMBER AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC ("Centipede") and the undersigned Justice Support Program member ("Member"). The Justice Support Program is an Active program of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, approved November 20, 2026.

1. What this program is

The Justice Support Program is a peer support program. Centipede staff are peers — people with lived experience who support, encourage, coach, mentor, and help Members navigate systems and connect to resources.

2. What this program is NOT

  • Centipede is not a law firm, and Centipede staff are not attorneys.
  • Nothing in this program is legal advice, and Centipede does not

represent any Member in court or in any legal matter.

  • This program is non-clinical. Centipede staff do not provide medical

or mental-health care, and staff do not speak for the court, for probation, or for any attorney.

  • Only the Member's own attorney (or public defender) can give the Member

legal advice. Only the court can decide the Member's case.

Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company. Program participation does not create an attorney–client relationship of any kind.

3. What Centipede will do

Within this program, Centipede will:

  • Track the Member's court dates and case stage (Awaiting Filing,

Arraignment, Pretrial, Hearing, Sentencing, Probation-Compliance, Resolved-Closed) in the Justice Support case tracker.

  • Remind the Member of upcoming court dates and check-ins, in the way the

Member chooses on the Court Date Reminder & Communication Consent form.

  • Accompany the Member to court at the San Joaquin County Superior Court

(Stockton Courthouse, 180 E. Weber Ave., Stockton, CA 95202) and to related appointments, when scheduled in advance and when staff are available. Accompaniment is moral support and navigation help only — Centipede staff do not speak on the Member's behalf in the courtroom.

  • Help the Member check court calendar and case information through the

court's public FullCourt portal (cms.sjcourts.org).

  • Help the Member connect to resources: identification documents, housing,

transportation, benefits, and other community supports in the Stockton and San Joaquin County area.

  • Help the Member keep track of paperwork and deadlines the Member must

meet, and help the Member prepare questions to take to the Member's attorney or probation officer.

4. What the Member agrees to

The Member agrees to:

  • Keep Centipede's contact information current — tell Centipede right away

if a phone number, address, or email changes.

  • Attend every court date, and tell Centipede about every court date,

continuance, or new date as soon as the Member learns of it.

  • Tell Centipede the truth about case information the Member shares, and

bring court paperwork (minute orders, notices, referrals) to Centipede so dates can be tracked correctly.

  • Treat Centipede staff, other members, and court staff with respect.
  • Understand that Centipede cannot change, delay, or influence any court

date, court order, or probation condition, and cannot promise any outcome in the Member's case.

5. The case tracker

The Member consents to Centipede keeping the Member's case information — name, case number, court dates, case stage, and support notes — in the Justice Support case tracker and the Member's program file, so Centipede can provide the services in this Agreement.

6. Confidentiality and its limits

Centipede keeps Member information confidential. Centipede will not release a Member's information to anyone outside the program except:

  • With the Member's signed Release of Information (ROI) naming who may

receive it;

  • When a judge-signed court order requires it (a subpoena alone is not

enough);

  • To the specific people the Member names on the Court Date Reminder &

Communication Consent form, and only about court dates and scheduling;

  • When the law requires reporting (for example, suspected abuse or neglect

of a child, an elder, or a dependent adult), or when there is a serious threat of harm.

7. Other company paperwork

This Agreement is the Justice Support Program's own agreement. The Member will also complete Centipede's standard company paperwork (intake packet, Release of Information, HIPAA authorization, and others as they apply). Those documents stand alongside this Agreement and are not replaced by it.

8. How participation ends

  • The Member may leave the program at any time, for any reason, by telling

Centipede. Leaving the program does not affect the Member's case or any court or probation obligation.

  • Centipede may end a Member's participation if the Member repeatedly

misses scheduled contacts, engages in threatening or abusive conduct toward staff or other members, or asks Centipede to do something unlawful or outside this program's role. Centipede will tell the Member when participation ends and why.

  • When a case reaches Resolved-Closed, Centipede will complete a Case

Closure Summary with the Member.

  • Ending participation never changes the Member's duty to appear in court

or to follow court orders and probation conditions.

9. Emergencies

This program is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.
