JUSTICE SUPPORT — MEMBER RIGHTS & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Justice Support Program is peer support. It is not legal advice and not legal representation. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company.

Your rights as a member

  • Respect. You will be treated with dignity and respect, without

regard to your charges, your record, your housing, or your income.

  • Honesty about our role. Staff will tell you plainly what we can and

cannot do. We are peers, not attorneys, and we will never pretend otherwise.

  • Privacy. Your information stays confidential. It is shared only with

your signed Release of Information, by judge-signed court order, with the people you name for court-date reminders, or when the law requires reporting for safety.

  • Your own decisions. Your case belongs to you and your attorney. We

support you; we do not decide for you.

  • Reminders your way. You choose how we remind you of court dates, and

you can change your choices at any time.

  • Ask questions. You may ask what is in your program file, and ask for

anything in it to be explained in plain language.

  • Leave at any time. You may end your participation whenever you

choose. Leaving never changes your court or probation obligations.

  • Speak up. If something is wrong, tell the program lead or the CEO's

office at (209) 871-3939. You may also contact the Board at board.centipedepeertopeer.llc@gmail.com. Speaking up will never be held against you.

Your responsibilities as a member

  • Show up. Attend every court date, probation check-in, and scheduled

appointment — or call ahead if a true emergency comes up.

  • Stay reachable. Keep your phone number and address current with us.
  • Share your dates. Tell us about every court date and every change

as soon as you learn of it, and bring us your court paperwork.

  • Be respectful. Treat staff, other members, and court staff with

respect — no threats, no abuse.

  • Be truthful. Give us accurate information so we can track your

dates and support you correctly.

  • Follow the court. Follow all court orders and probation conditions.

Centipede cannot change them, excuse them, or speak for you about them.

Emergencies

This program is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.
