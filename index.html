Stockton Warming Centers — Winter

When winter nights turn dangerously cold, the City of Stockton opens overnight warming centers for anyone who needs a warm, safe place to rest. The City activates the centers during severe cold weather and announces each opening on the City’s website and social media — check the City of Stockton’s Severe Weather Resources page for the current activation status before you go.

These are City of Stockton warming centers. Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC does not run these centers — we are sharing this information to help people find a warm, safe place to rest.

Stribley Community Center

1760 E. Sonora St, Stockton, CA 95205

Hours when activated

Open overnight, 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM (per the City’s Severe Weather Resources page).

What to expect

A heated indoor resting space with restrooms, water, and snacks. Warming centers are a warm place to rest overnight — they are not full shelters and do not provide meals or medical care.

Open to anyone who is without adequate heat or protection from the cold. No sign-up is mentioned by the City; availability can change with weather conditions and usage.

What inspired me to start Centipede Peer To Peer

Centipede Peer To Peer grew out of my own life experiences — the struggles I’ve faced, the people who lifted me up when I had nothing, and the times I had to rebuild myself from scratch. I know what it’s like to feel overwhelmed, stuck, to fight addiction, mental health challenges, or homelessness, and to believe the world has written you off. I also know the power of just one person showing up, listening, and believing in you — how that kind of support can change everything.

I started Centipede Peer To Peer to offer the kind of help I wish more people could find: real support from people who truly understand, not because they studied it, but because they’ve lived it. My journey taught me that people don’t need perfection — they need presence. They need someone who won’t judge, rush, or talk down to them. Someone who sees their potential even when they can’t. That belief is at the heart of everything we do here.

“Levi Spears Sr. exemplifies true leadership and compassion. His dedication to Centipede Peer To Peer is transforming lives and building a stronger community.”

Our core values and beliefs

At Centipede Peer To Peer, our foundation is built on unwavering principles that guide every interaction. First, we believe every person has worth, no matter their situation. No one is “too far gone” or “beyond help”; everyone deserves dignity, respect, and a chance to rebuild. Second, lived experience is a gift, not a shame. Our team comprises individuals who have walked similar paths, offering understanding that textbooks simply cannot.

Healing starts with connection, not judgment or labels. We prioritize real relationships over clinical distance, fostering an environment where people feel seen and heard. Lastly, we meet people where they are, literally and emotionally, providing support on the streets, in shelters, or wherever it is needed most. Everyone moves at their own pace, and we honor that journey without pressure.

How we hope to make a real difference

Centipede Peer To Peer was created with one purpose: to change lives through connection, compassion, and consistent support. Our hope is to make a real, lasting impact in two places — in the lives of the people we help and in the community as a whole. We help people feel seen, heard, and valued, knowing that many we meet feel invisible or forgotten.

We guide individuals toward stability and hope, whether they are struggling with addiction, mental-health challenges, homelessness, or trauma, by supporting them in taking the next step, no matter how small. We actively break cycles of isolation, fostering connection, community, and a sense of belonging. Ultimately, we support recovery and life rebuilding, helping people find resources, build coping skills, and reclaim their lives.

Why Centipede Has a Board

Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California limited liability company. The law does not require an LLC to maintain a board of directors. We maintain one deliberately, as a matter of governance.

A company that works in the open owes the public more than good intentions. It owes structure. Our board provides independent oversight of the company’s programs, finances, and record; reviews performance against stated standards; and ensures that every major decision is examined, documented, and defensible — before it is carried out, not after it is questioned.

The board’s charge is accountability in four directions: to our clients, whose dignity and safety govern our work; to our partners, including county agencies and law enforcement, who rely on our word; to our funders, whose resources must be accounted for to the dollar; and to the Stockton community, whose trust is earned in public or not at all.

Board service at Centipede is not ceremonial. Directors review outreach results, examine the written record, challenge assumptions, and hold management — including the Chief Executive Officer — to the company’s own standards: non-clinical peer support, honestly labeled programs, clean books, and decisions that can survive daylight.

That is why an LLC has a board at Centipede. Not because the law demands it. Because the work does.

Privacy

Privacy & HIPAA Compliance

Your information is yours. This page states, in plain terms, the standard Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC holds itself to for every record we keep and every conversation we have — and the rights you hold over your own information.

Our Commitment

Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company. We provide non-clinical peer support and outreach — we are not a hospital, a health plan, a treatment facility, or a therapy practice. We follow the privacy and security standards of the federal Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) for all client records, together with California’s Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA), California Civil Code § 56 et seq. Where California law is stricter than federal law, the stricter California standard is the one we follow.

This standard applies to information coming to us and to information going out — in both directions, without exception.

What This Covers

This protection is not limited to paperwork or stored records. It covers anything a member shares with us in the course of peer support and outreach: what is said in conversation, on phone calls, and in messages, and anything we discuss with an agency on a member’s behalf — what is said, to whom, and how.

We do not discuss our members with anyone outside serving that member’s own needs. Conversations about a member happen only for that member — their recovery, their connections, and what they need.

What a member tells us is shared with an agency or with anyone else only with the member’s authorization, or under a court order — never on a request alone.

How We Protect Your Information

• Client records are kept locked. Access is limited: a person’s information is seen only by someone who has both a genuine need to see it and clear authorization to do so.
• Information is never shared out of curiosity, familiarity, or pressure. There are no exceptions.
• We use and share only the information needed for the purpose at hand.
• We do not give client information to any government body — county, state, or federal — on a request alone. Information is released only with the member’s own signed Release of Information, or under a court order, and then only what the order covers, through the proper channel, fully documented, with our Chief Executive Officer informed.

Your Rights

Under HIPAA, you have the right to:

• Ask to see and get a copy of your records.
• Ask for corrections to be made to your information.
• Know how your information may be used and shared — this page is that notice.
• Get a report of when and why your information was shared, for the disclosures the law covers.
• File a complaint with us, or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, if you believe your rights have been denied or your information is not being protected.

You will never be retaliated against for filing a complaint or for asking about your own information.

Statistics

Any numbers we publish about our work are combined totals drawn from many people’s records. They cannot be traced back to any one person, and no individual can be identified from them.

If Something Goes Wrong

If your information is ever accessed or shared in a way the law does not permit, we will notify you as HIPAA and California law require.
