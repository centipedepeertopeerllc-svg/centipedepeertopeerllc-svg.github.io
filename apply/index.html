Work With Us — Apply

Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC is a California for-profit limited liability company providing non-clinical peer support and homeless outreach in Stockton, California. Our team supports, encourages, coaches, mentors, and connects people to resources — peer to peer, with lived experience at the center.

There are two doors below. Employment is paid work on our team. Volunteering is unpaid service alongside our team, and it is separate from employment. Choose the door that fits you — each one opens our official application form in a new tab.

Each application ends with an electronic signature.

Please know: every application is reviewed by the company. Applying does not guarantee a position or a volunteer placement. And Centipede never asks for a Social Security number, a date of birth, or an ID number on these forms or anywhere online — if anyone asks you for those in our name, do not provide them; call us at (209) 871-3939.
