    Intake — before membership

    Intake comes first (CPP-POL-2026-043). No one becomes a member of Centipede Peer to Peer, LLC, or of any of its programs, until an intake is completed and approved here. An approved intake creates the person's record in the Clients list; the client's locked binder remains the official record.

    The information every intake collects

    • About you
    • How to reach you
    • Living situation
    • Emergency contact
    • How you found us
    • Income and benefits
    • Documents you hold — never any ID numbers
    • Health and support needs — peer support only, nothing clinical
    • Safety and urgent needs
    • Justice — only if it applies
    • Veterans — only if it applies
    • Your goals and preferences
    • Contact permissions
    • Intake follow-up
    • What they need help with, and the programs they are asking about

    The documents needed to approve an intake

    • Required: government photo ID — a copy is held in the binder; the ID number is never recorded
    • Required: signed Release of Information / consent
    • Required: signed Intake & Participation Agreement (peer support, non-clinical)
    • Insurance card copy — or the person is routed to the Clients Without Insurance list
    • Proof of living situation where it exists (shelter letter, HMIS printout) — a signed self-declaration stands when no document exists
    • Justice Support: court paperwork and attorney-of-record information
    • Veterans Program: DD-214 or VA documentation of service

    An intake can be approved only when the three required documents are in. Everything else is marked as it stands — received, self-declared, or still to come — and followed up.

    Start an intake — every question

    Ask what applies and leave the rest blank — a blank is an honest answer, never a guess. Identification numbers are never asked for and never recorded: no Social Security number, no ID card number, no Veteran ID Card number.
